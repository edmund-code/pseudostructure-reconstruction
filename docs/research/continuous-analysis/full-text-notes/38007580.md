## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic (PSTS, the part the review cites): per spot, a "modified" diffusion pseudotime (DPT) from a root chosen semi-automatically (user picks the root cluster; CytoTRACE score picks the spot). Between sub-clusters, a pseudo-time-space distance dPTS = weighted combination (weight omega) of an expression distance (mean cosine distance between PCA vectors of spots) and the physical distance between cluster centroids. A directed bipartite "spatial-PAGA" graph is optimised by a minimum directed spanning tree (Chu-Liu/Edmonds); omega is tuned by Laplacian spectral distances to the expression-only and space-only graphs (optimal 0.46-0.51 in three datasets). Morphology (H&E ResNet50 features) enters only through the optional stSME normalisation/imputation step before clustering, not through dPTS (Methods "Pseudo-time-space algorithm", "stSME").
- Null / calibration / significance: transition genes = Spearman correlation of expression with PSTS value along a clade, BH-adjusted P < 0.05 and |rho| > 0.3, spots as units (Methods "Detection of trajectory-based transition markers"); then Enrichr/gseapy on the top 100 up and down genes. Trajectory quality judged by variograms of pseudotime values over tissue space (Fig. 2i). No calibration of the gene test is shown.
- Replicates: one section at a time. For several sections: register into a common coordinate frame and merge, or run PSTS per section and intersect transition genes (Results; Methods "Spatial trajectory inference by PSTS from multiple datasets"; Fig. S13). Demonstrated only with two adjacent sections of one breast-cancer block.
- Validation: mouse traumatic brain injury Visium (3 dpi; microglia spots filtered by Fcrls/Tmem119; histology of microglia density and size over 6 time points, n=4 per time point); sci-Space E14 embryonic brain; Visium breast cancer DCIS->IDC; comparisons with Slingshot, Monocle3, SpaceFlow by variograms and plausibility. CCI (SCTP) and imputation (stSME) are validated separately (simulation, RNAscope, ARI).
- Plot types: trajectory arrows overlaid on tissue with PSTS colour (Fig. 2b, 3b,e); clustering maps; transition-gene bar/ranked plots (Fig. 2d); pathway enrichment (Fig. 2e); variograms (Fig. 2i); spatial-PAGA graphs (Fig. 3c); chord plots and network diagrams for CCI (Figs 4-5).
- Software: Python stLearn (github.com/BiomedicalMachineLearning/stLearn; stlearn.readthedocs.io), plus i-stLearn web app.
- Limitations (authors): 2-D sections; few longitudinal datasets; spatial weighting can over-fit to nearest clusters. For us: the coordinate is built from tissue x/y plus expression at cluster level; gene tests are spot-level Spearman correlations without replicates.
