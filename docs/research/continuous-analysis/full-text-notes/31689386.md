## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Design: 2 male and 2 female C57BL/6J mice (8-10 weeks); each kidney cut into cortex (zone 1), outer medulla (zone 2) and inner medulla (zone 3) before cold-protease dissociation; 12 libraries (10x v2); 31,265 cells after QC (Results "Anatomy directed profiling"; STAR Methods).
- Analysis: Seurat 2.3.4 graph clustering (30 PCs, resolution 1.0) into 30 clusters; nephron and ureteric subsets re-clustered (18 nephron clusters; 16 UE clusters); markers by Wilcoxon (FindAllMarkers). Zone of origin and sex used to annotate clusters (stacked bar plots, Figs. 1D-E, 2D-E). PANTHER GO for sex-biased PT sets.
- PT findings: all PT clusters separate by sex; segment markers that are similar between sexes: Spp2/Slc5a2 (S1), Slc34a1 (S1-2), Slc22a7 (S3) (Fig. 2F). "The highest disparity in gene activity between the sexes was in the S3 region" (e.g. Slc7a12 female, Cyp7b1 male in Slc22a7+ S3; Cyp2e1 male and Cyp4a14 female in S2), validated by RNAscope and qPCR (n = 1 per assay) (Fig. 2G-I). "S2 and S3 sex-specific expression domains form a tight, non-overlapping boundary at the S2-S3 junction" (Supp. Fig. S2D, not read). Male S3 cells were "markedly underrepresented" (~10% of expected), which the authors attribute to stress-induced cell death during dissociation.
- Other results: thin-limb cell types differ between juxtamedullary (early-born, Wnt4 lineage at E15.5) and cortical (P2) nephrons (Fig. 3); collecting-duct principal-cell diversity along the cortico-medullary axis and by sex, with female replicates diverging more (Fig. 4); dual nephron/ureteric origin of cortical PC-like and intercalated cells by lineage tracing (Fig. 5).
- Null / replicate unit: cluster-level Wilcoxon markers across cells; replicates shown only as cluster composition ("Replicates were similarly represented in most clusters", Supp. Fig. S1E); no specimen-level test.
- Plot types: tSNE coloured by cluster, sex or zone; stacked bar plots of cluster composition by sex and zone; violin and dot plots of markers; RNAscope images; metacell heatmaps on juxtamedullary and cortical nephron schematics in the Kidney Cell Explorer (Fig. 6).
- Software: Cell Ranger 2.0, Seurat 2.3.4, PANTHER; Kidney Cell Explorer (Shiny, VisCello) https://cello.shinyapps.io/kidneycellexplorer/; GEO GSE129798.
- Limitations (authors): podocytes under-represented; non-epithelial recovery not optimised; species differences expected. For OUR design: dissociation biases cell recovery along the PT (male S3 loss); position is discrete (zone of dissection, clusters).

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
Useful as a source of priors and positive controls, not methods. Our cohort is all male, so male-specific PT genes (e.g. Cyp7b1 in S3, Cyp2e1 and Acsm3 extending into S2) are expected in mouse profiles and should not be read as species or injury effects; genes with a reported sharp S2/S3 boundary are candidate controls for any step-at-boundary model.
