## What the paper actually does (from the preprint)
- Design: male mice, APAP 300 mg/kg after 12 h fast, all injections at the same circadian time. Bulk RNA-seq (mcSCRB-seq) of 29 livers: 3 mice per time point at 6 h, 24 h, 48 h, 72 h, 96 h, 1 w, 1 m plus saline controls (Methods "APAP injections"). scRNA-seq (10x v3.1 with hashing) of 20,587 cells from 19 mice at control, 24 h, 48 h, 72 h, 96 h, 1 w (2-4 mice per time point) (Results; Fig. 1).
- Hepatocyte data are thin: 532 cells from 2 mice at 48 h and 239 from 2 mice at 72 h, plus 2 control mice re-used from Droin 2021 (2,770 cells in total) (Methods "Hepatocyte Processing"). HSC controls and 20 h were re-used from Kolodziejczyk 2020 (different APAP dose and fasting) (Methods "Hepatic stellate cell cluster analysis").
- Coordinate: per hepatocyte, pLM/(cLM + pLM) from 19 pericentral and 31 periportal landmark genes (Droin's lists), each normalised to cell UMI sum and to its maximum. Cells are then cut into three zones by tertiles of the coordinate within each time point (Methods "Inferring lobule spatial coordinates"). For HSCs the landmark genes are re-derived at each time point from correlation with Ngfr, because activation changes zonated genes; endothelial zones from markers plus Halpern 2018 landmarks.
- Per-gene summaries: zone x time means and SEM over cells (Tables S4-S6; Figs. 4C-I, 5D).
- Key analyses: (1) the distribution of the coordinate per time point - pericentral hepatocytes depleted at 48 h, near control at 72 h (Fig. 2F); (2) "interface hepatocytes" (coordinate below the control median, 0.73) at 48 h and 72 h compared with control hepatocytes resampled by inverse-transform sampling to match their coordinate distribution (Methods "Comparing interface hepatocytes and control"; Fig. 3A-B: >= 2-fold, FDR 0.01); (3) GSEA (KEGG, Hallmark) on genes ranked by correlation with an Afp-based meta-signature (Fig. 3E); (4) proliferation score by zone and time, Kruskal-Wallis across cells (Fig. 2K).
- Null / replicate unit: cells. Kruskal-Wallis and FDR across cells; the morphology test is a paired sign-rank on 60 cell pairs from 3 + 3 mice (Fig. 3G). Bulk analyses use mice (Spearman distances, Fig. 2A; CIBERSORTx fractions, Fig. S1C).
- Biology: hepatocytes proliferate across the whole lobule; interface hepatocytes at the regenerating front transiently express fetal/onco-fetal genes (Afp, Cdh17, Spp1), Wnt targets (Lgr5, Axin2), ribosome and proteasome genes, and have an elongated, less circular shape; pericentral HSCs make ECM while periportal HSCs proliferate; MHC and lymphocyte-attracting chemokines fall in all cell types; zonation is restored by 96 h (Results; Discussion; Fig. 7).
- Plot types: coordinate distributions per time point (Fig. 2F); UMAP coloured by coordinate (Fig. 2E); zone-stratified time-course lines (mean +- SE) per gene (Figs. 4C-I, 5D, S4B); regenerating-vs-matched-control expression scatter (Fig. 3A-B); GSEA NES dot plot (Fig. 3E); smFISH lobule scans.
- Software: Cell Ranger 3.1, Seurat 3.1.5 (SCTransform, integration), CIBERSORTx, GSEA preranked; "All code used will be available from the authors upon request"; data on Zenodo (doi 10.5281/zenodo.5172137).
- Limitations (authors): mRNA dynamics within 4 days are shorter than protein lifetimes; only pericentral damage studied (Discussion). Limitations for OUR design: 2 injured mice per time point for hepatocytes, cell-level tests, three tertile zones only, and a coordinate built from landmark genes that injury itself reprograms.

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the preprint)
Useful for AKI design, not for statistics. Two transferable ideas: plot unit density along the axis per condition before comparing expression, and compare injured units with control units matched on coordinate distribution. One warning that the review lacks: if injury changes the genes that build the coordinate, injured units can be mispositioned; our IR units' pseudospace positions should be checked against a coordinate that excludes injury-responsive genes.
