## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Samples: 16 human liver samples from 16 individuals: 8 live healthy donors (LHD, M1-M8; 3 with mild steatosis) and 8 adjacent-normal resections (P). The main atlas is standard 10x Visium (fresh-frozen, polyA capture; 44,243 spots kept). Visium HD (FFPE, probe-based) only on 3 LHDs (M1, M2, M6) as validation (Spearman R = 0.7 with Visium zonation profiles, Ext. Data Fig. 3) and to build a segmented single-cell atlas for NPC landmark genes (M2, M6). MERFISH on 2 (M5, M8; R = 0.49 and 0.57). snRNA-seq on 4 (M5-M8). PhenoCycler on 6. Other species (Visium): mouse n = 6 from a published dataset (GEO GSE248077), wild boar 2, pig 3, cow 2 (newly generated).
- Coordinate: per human Visium spot, landmark score = sum(periportal landmarks) / (sum periportal + sum pericentral), with the 20 genes most correlated and anticorrelated with CYP2E1 as landmark sets (gene lists in Methods); binned into 8 equal-percentile zones; median filter over spots within 150 um. Other species: 6 zones; landmark genes for mouse and cow, distance to portal fibrotic septa for pig and boar.
- Per-gene summaries (Methods "Human portal-central axis spot annotation"): zonation P by Kruskal-Wallis across zones with spots as units, BH (1,141 of 1,724 hepatocyte genes q<0.25); centre of mass per orthologous gene per sample; gene zonation score = linear combination of argmax zone and COM; GSEA (KEGG, Hallmark) on that score (Ext. Data Fig. 5a); mid-lobule score = max in layers 4-6 / max elsewhere (Ext. Data Fig. 5e); portal bias = log2(expression in the most periportal zone / most pericentral zone). For ligands, receptors and TFs, per-patient zonation P values are combined by Fisher's method (Ext. Data Fig. 5b-d).
- Cross-species: human vs mouse ortholog scatter of portal bias, Spearman R = 0.15 (Fig. 3a); GSEA of human-minus-mouse portal-bias differences (Fig. 3c); portal bias of summed cell-type marker profiles (Fig. 3d); 12,410 1:1 orthologues across 5 species, 1,249 hepatocyte genes; "one-tailed rank-sum P < 1.45e-6 for all species" comparing the distribution of portal biases ACROSS GENES between human and each species, with species profiles taken as weighted means over samples (Fig. 3h; dots are per-sample means for display). Human samples in the cross-species analysis: Methods says n = 7 (steatosis < 10%); Fig. 3h legend says n = 5 (M4-M8) - an internal inconsistency.
- Other analyses: LHD vs adjacent normal pseudo-bulk (rank-sum DE; a regression of pseudo-bulk expression on sample type, histology, age and sex, called "linear mixed regression" in the text but described as multilinear regression in Ext. Data Fig. 1 legend); NPC zonation by assigning snRNA-seq nuclei to 8 layers with Visium HD-derived landmarks; ligand-receptor zonation correlation; early steatosis by H&E pixel-classified lipid content per spot (QuPath) and k-means of gene profiles across lipid bins.
- Plot types: zone-coloured H&E maps; max-normalised zonation heat maps sorted by COM (Fig. 2b); mean +/- s.e.m. zonation profiles across samples (Fig. 2c, 3b); portal-bias ortholog scatter (Fig. 3a); GSEA bars; marker-set portal-bias bars with discordant sets in red (Fig. 3d); per-species portal-bias box plots (Fig. 3h); lipid-bin trajectories with per-patient lines (Fig. 5f,g).
- Software: MATLAB (code github.com/OranYak/Human-liver; data on Zenodo 17735506, 17735558, 17735587); web app shalevapps.weizmann.ac.il/liver_app.
- Limitations (stated or evident): small n per comparison (5-7 non-steatotic LHD); Kruskal-Wallis with spots as units; the cross-species rank-sum treats genes as the units. For us: the cross-species comparison is not on a species-specific probe panel (polyA Visium in all species); only 3 human samples on Visium HD.

## Fit to our design
High for figures and summaries (within-species log end-ratio, per-sample COM, COM-sorted heat maps, ortholog scatter with conserved and reversed anchors, honest weak cross-species correlation). Medium-low for inference: species tests are gene-level rank-sums on averaged profiles and zonation tests use spots as units. Our specimen-level calibration and the probe-panel offset are problems this paper does not address.
