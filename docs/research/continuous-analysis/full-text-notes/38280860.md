## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic
  - Three-step workflow on one (or per-condition) trajectory (Results "General model and workflow"): Step 1 differential topology; Step 2 differential progression (pseudotime distributions) and differential fate selection (lineage-weight distributions); Step 3 gene-level conditionTest.
  - Imbalance score (Methods "Imbalance score"): for each cell, a kNN graph (Euclidean, in the reduced-dimensional space X such as PCA/UMAP), a multinomial-test z-score of the neighbours' condition labels against the global condition proportions (Supplementary S-1.2), then smoothed with cubic splines per dimension; defaults k = 10, s = 10. Explicitly "an exploratory tool". It lives on the embedding, not on pseudotime.
  - topologyTest (Methods "Differential topology"): fit one trajectory per condition; permute condition labels r = 100 times and refit; compare the per-cell pseudotime distribution with the (averaged) permuted ones by weighted KS (L = 1), kernel two-sample test (C = 2) or classifier test; usually tested against an effect-size threshold so that a common trajectory is favoured. Only slingshot and TSCAN (fixed skeleton).
  - progressionTest: H0 equal pseudotime distributions between conditions, per lineage (weighted KS, Monahan weights with effective sample sizes) or global (default for L > 1: Stouffer combination of lineage KS p-values weighted by lineage weight sums; or classifier/kernel test). fateSelectionTest: H0 equal lineage-weight distributions; default classifier test with random forests.
  - Gene model (Methods "Testing for differential expression", Eq. 11): tradeSeq NB-GAM with lineage x condition smoothers s_jlc (K cubic basis functions), an optional additional design matrix U (e.g. batch) and log library size offset; fitted with mgcv. conditionTest: Wald test of H0 "the smoothers are identical across conditions" (beta_jlc1k = beta_jlc2k for all k, l), usually run against a log-fold-change threshold (TGF-β and Fibrosis: threshold 2).
- Null / calibration / significance
  - All tests treat cells as i.i.d.; the authors state the assumptions are violated (pseudotimes estimated from all cells) and say they view p-values "as useful numerical summaries" rather than with "strong probabilistic interpretations" (Discussion; Methods end of "Testing for differential expression").
  - Supplementary S-2.4 (Fig. S8-S9): with random condition labels on the TGF-β data, conditionTest p-values are valid (conservative) at all thresholds, but "when testing against a null of no change (log-fold-change of 0), conditionTest with BH-correction does not properly control the FDR at any level"; with a threshold of log(1.2) or more it does.
  - Replicate unit = cell. No sample/replicate random effect. Batch can enter U "if batches are not fully confounded with conditions" (Discussion). Supplementary S-1.1.3: when each batch contains every condition, "distinct" (permutation using multiple batches) or a per-batch topologyTest combined by Stouffer.
- Validation: dyngen simulations (2, 3 or 5 lineages; 2 or 3 conditions; knock-out multiplier m; subsamples of n = 2,000 cells, 50-100 per setting), comparing progression/fateSelection tests to milo and DAseq by TNR, PPV, TPR, NPV, F1 (Fig. 2e); robustness to noisy pseudotime/lineage assignment (Fig. S4) and to the Step-1 decision (Fig. S5). Case studies: TGF-β EMT (9,268 cells; topologyTest p = 0.38; 1,993 DE genes in Table 2), lung fibrosis (14,462 cells; 3 DE genes), TCDD liver hepatocytes along the central-portal axis (9,951 cells; supplement S-2.7), KRAS (10,177 cells, 3 conditions).
- Spatial precedent inside the paper (Supplementary S-2.7, Fig. S13-S14): TCDD-treated vs control mouse liver hepatocytes on a central -> portal trajectory; progressionTest found TCDD cells skewed toward the central start (p ≤ 2.2e-16), matching the known periportal toxicity; conditionTest found 2,114 DE genes (Table 2 says 2,144), with zonal genes (Halpern) twice as frequent among DE genes. Authors note Steps 2-3 are invariant to reversing the spatial axis.
- Plot types: imbalance-score map on the UMAP (Fig. 3c, 5c); per-condition pseudotime density (Fig. 3e, 5f); lineage-weight distributions (Fig. 5e); top conditionTest genes, counts with per-condition smoothers (Fig. 3f); pseudocolour image of fitted values for DE genes in both conditions, scaled to [0,1] per gene (Fig. 3g, S12e, S14d, S17d); milo/DAseq neighbourhood plots for comparison (Fig. 4b-c).
- Software: R/Bioconductor condiments (https://github.com/HectorRDB/condiments); distribution tests in the CRAN package Ecume; reproduction vignettes https://hectorrdb.github.io/condimentsPaper.
- Limitations stated: i.i.d. assumptions violated ("double-dipping" judged of limited impact in simulations); batch confounded with condition cannot be corrected; topologyTest restricted to slingshot/TSCAN; p-values are summaries, not calibrated inference.
- Limitations for OUR design: no specimen level at all - with 2 vs 2 mice the condition effect is fully confounded with specimen identity, which is exactly the setting Lamian showed gives thousands of false calls (Lamian null: condiments 8,753); KS on thousands of units will reject for any specimen difference.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Useful for two ideas only: test whether IR shifts the distribution of units along the PT axis before comparing expression (the TCDD liver case is a spatial precedent), and the side-by-side [0,1]-scaled fitted-value image. Its tests are cell-level, without a specimen term, and the authors call the p-values numerical summaries; at 2 vs 2 condition is confounded with specimen, so none of its p-values transfer. The NB-GAM conditionTest is tradeSeq with condition smoothers; our per-specimen NB curves already cover it.
