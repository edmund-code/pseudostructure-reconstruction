## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model: Bayesian nonlinear factor analysis that infers cell pseudotimes from a small marker panel (5-12 genes; 374 in the cell-cycle example). Each gene's mean along pseudotime is either a sigmoid (activation strength k, activation time t0, peak level eta; same form as switchde) or a Gaussian-shaped transient (peak time p, width b, fixed lambda = 10). The shape of each gene must be specified by the user in advance (Section 4.3: "the requirement to pre-specify genes as having switch-like or transient behaviour"); informative priors on k, t0, p, b are optional.
- Likelihood: log expression scaled per gene to a half-peak of 1; zero-inflated mixture with a logistic dropout model and a Student-t non-zero component with a mean-variance relation sigma^2 = (1 + phi) mu + epsilon.
- Inference: Stan NUTS (HMC); stochastic variational inference in Edward/TensorFlow ("ouijaflow") for large panels.
- Gene ordering test: posterior of t_A - t_B; "if both the lower and upper bounds of the 95% posterior credible interval fall outside 0 we say the two genes are regulated at significantly different times" (Section 4.2, Fig. 3D). Applies to switch times and peak times.
- Metastable states: a cell x cell "consistency" matrix of posterior P(cell i before cell j) across MCMC draws; PCA to one component; Gaussian mixture with the number of states chosen by BIC (Section 4.4, Fig. 4A). These states depend on uncertainty in the cell ordering.
- Null / calibration: Bayesian credible intervals only; no FDR.
- Replicates: none; cells are observations.
- Validation: correlation of marker-based pseudotime with Monocle 2, DPT and TSCAN transcriptome-wide pseudotimes on five datasets (mostly > 0.75; poor on Shin 2015 because markers were redundant); robustness to misspecifying switch vs transient in simulation (median correlation > 0.9 with 4 switch + 4 transient genes modelled as all switch); recovery of EC / pre-HSC / HSC states (Zhou 2016); cell-cycle ordering of 1,008 HSCs.
- Plots: heat map of markers ordered by posterior switch time (Fig. 3A); posterior switch and peak times with 95% HPD intervals, coloured up/down (Fig. 3B,C); pairwise timing-difference plot (Fig. 3D); ordering-consistency heat map (Fig. 4A); profiles with metastable-state background colours (Fig. 4C); genes ordered by activation time (Fig. 5F).
- Software: R `ouija` (github.com/kieranrcampbell/ouija), Python `ouijaflow`.
- Limitations stated: needs informative, non-redundant markers; shape must be pre-specified. Limitations for us: our coordinate is fixed (no latent pseudotime to infer); cells as replicates; Student-t on log expression, not NB with offset.

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
Useful for two pieces: a parametric bump (peak, width) to sit beside the sigmoid, and the credible-interval test for "A before B" on switch or peak positions. With specimens as replicates, the analogous test is whether specimen-level differences in edge positions agree in sign across specimens, not a cell-level posterior.
