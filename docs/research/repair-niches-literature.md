# Repair niches after IRI at 12 h–2 d: what the literature predicts (scout, 2026-10-18)

A review for notebook 110. Of 38 papers retrieved, 33 were read in full (methods, results and figure legends); 5 are
abstract-only and are marked. The source texts stayed in local scratch and are not in the repository.

## The most decisive paper

**Bordoni et al. 2023, Nat Commun 14:4433** (PMC10362041) [full text]. Partial IRI with serial intravital
two-photon imaging and a CycB1-GFP reporter.
- **Necrosis by segment:** PT-S1 26.2% > PT-S2 15.5% > DCT/CD 2.9% PI⁺ nuclei. Deep S3 could not be imaged.
- **Timing:** proliferation peaks at day 3 and is significant from day 1 in IR regions.
- **Proliferation sits next to death:** 2.27 ± 0.15% GFP⁺ within 25 µm of necrotic nuclei, against 1.62 ± 0.14% beyond
  (p < 0.001).
- **A second wave runs within the nephron:** casts flow downstream and drive proliferation in non-necrotic S2 24 h
  later. Single-tubule laser injury proves it: casted S2 8.35% against uncasted 0.46%. Tubules 0–35 µm from the laser
  site were no more proliferative than those 85–135 µm away.
- **Proliferation does not mean recovery:** tubules that later atrophied had more GFP⁺ cells.

## Intrinsic repair: timing, segment, clustering

- **Humphreys 2008 / 2011** [full text]:
  - S3 Ki67 0.4% → 47.4% at day 2;
  - IdU detected from 24 h, 26.9% at 48 h;
  - 72–75% of dividing cells are Kim-1⁺, and 71.9% have lost basolateral Na/K-ATPase: "injured and dedifferentiated
    rather than uninjured bystanders".
- **Witzgall 1994** (rat) [full text]:
  - PCNA⁺ ≈ 80% in the outer stripe against ≈ 23% in cortex at 48 h;
  - dedifferentiation is all-or-none per tubule (vimentin in > 90% of cells of a positive tubule);
  - clusterin⁺ cells are never PCNA⁺.
- **Kusaba 2014** [full text]: the number of divisions is governed by the degree of injury.
- **Berger 2014** [full text]:
  - "tubular cells respond to injury in a graded fashion";
  - "one would expect EGFP-positive cells to be arranged in clusters … which was not the case".
- **Kirita 2020** [full text]:
  - the cells of the injured clusters come from 4 h and 12 h;
  - a cycling "repairing PT" arises at 2 d;
  - FR-PTC appear "within Kim1-positive injured tubule in a scattered manner at 2 d";
  - cortical fibroblast activation peaks at day 2.
- **Chang-Panesso 2019** [full text]: the proliferation wave peaks at 48 h in the outer stripe, and 87% of clones are
  single cells at day 2.
- **Kumar 2015** [full text]: at 48 h, 89% of SOX9⁺ cells are HAVCR1⁺ and 40% are Ki67⁺. Contiguous clusters
  appear only by 4 weeks.
- **Lazzeri 2018** [full text]: Ki-67 47% but FUCCI S/G2/M only 13% at day 2, so Ki-67 overstates division. Disputed
  by Chang-Panesso.
- **Rinkevich 2014** [full text]: clones "did not cross to adjacent segments within a nephron … nor to neighboring
  nephrons".

## Immune and vascular crosstalk

- **Lee 2011** [full text]:
  - F4/80⁺ influx from day 1 around injured outer-medullary tubules;
  - proliferation peaks at day 3;
  - depleting macrophages at 48–72 h cut Ki-67⁺ cells 45% at day 5.
- **Lin 2010** [full text]: repair begins on day 2. Macrophage Wnt7b changes G2 transit, not cycle entry.
- **Zhang 2012** [full text]: M1 at 18 h and M2 at 72 h. Macrophage proliferation rises 0.19% → 2.5% of kidney cells
  by day 3.
- **Melo Ferreira 2021** [full text, n = 1 section at 6 h]:
  - Havcr1 lies in the outer stripe;
  - 52.2% of infiltrating neutrophils sit in the outer stripe;
  - Atf3 is the tubule-side correlate.
- **Dixon 2022** [full text]: "Kim-1 positive tubules at 6 weeks … have increased F4/80 positive signal compared with
  sham and 12-hour time points". This is a 12 h negative.
- **Cheung 2022** [full text]: resident macrophages redistribute within 12 h rather than accumulate.
- **Basile 2011** [full text]:
  - no endothelial proliferation in the first 2 days;
  - lectin perfusion is undetectable in the outer stripe at 24 h;
  - capillary loss appears at day 7.
- **Kapitsinou 2014** [full text]: E-selectin peaks at 2 h and VCAM1 rises by day 3. Capillary density is unchanged at
  day 9.

## Spatial transcriptomics

- **Xuanyuan 2025** [full text, n ≈ 1 mouse per time point]:
  - injured PT is 33.2% of cells at 12 h;
  - the healthy-S3 neighbourhood is absent at 12 h and 2 d;
  - at 12 h no neighbourhood has a raised cell-cycle score;
  - at 2 d all do, the injured-PT neighbourhood most;
  - fibroblasts rise 7.1% → 16.1% at 2 d while immune cells stay at 3.3%.
- **Wu 2024** [full text]: injured PT spreads through the cortex early, fibroblasts proliferate with a day-2 peak, and
  no neutrophil markers are on the panel.
- **Polonsky 2024** [full text, day 28]:
  - microenvironments within 30 µm, stable from 10 to 50 µm;
  - a within-class neighbourhood effect: injury genes are higher in injured cells inside the injured
    microenvironment;
  - Crlf1⁺ fibroblasts decay with distance from injured cells.
- **Zhang YL 2024** [full text]: the outer stripe is most injured, with macrophages and neutrophils accumulating there
  at day 1.
- **Lake 2023** [full text]:
  - human niches: neutrophil OR 4.4–4.6 near altered PT spots, MAC-M2 not significant;
  - in the mouse IRI projection, cycling nuclei are 0.7% at 12 h and 10.5% at 2 d.
- **Baker 2026** [full text, human ATI biopsies about 6–8 d]:
  - injured PT has more CD206⁺ macrophage neighbours and fewer endothelial neighbours;
  - k-NN with k = 5 at a 16 µm centroid spacing;
  - GLMM clustered by patient.
- **Abstract only:** Rietjens 2026, Melkonian 2025, Lake 2025 bioRxiv, Matsuura Kidney Week 2026, Basile 2001.

## Gap

No mouse or human study quantifies whether proliferating tubular cells are enriched near injured tubules or near
particular interstitial cells at 12 h–2 d.

## Marker notes for a Visium HD mouse panel

- **Avoid as lineage markers:**
  - Lcn2 is tubular, not neutrophil, in kidney;
  - Vcam1 is in injured PT, activated endothelium and parietal epithelium;
  - Spp1 and C3 are in PT and in leukocytes.
- **Score, never single genes:** Adgre1 and Cd68 are sparse.
- **Unmeasurable:** T and B cells.

## Controls the protocol should name

- The compartment of CYC (interior against edge). Cycling interstitial cells are abundant at 2 d.
- Entry against division: E2F against G2M.
- Own injury is near-saturated, so include a nonlinear own-injury term.
- The specimen is the replication unit.
- Sensitivity to k and radius.
- Position and depth are covariates only.
