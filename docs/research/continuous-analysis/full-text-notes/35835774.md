## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model: a spatial expression graph (alpha-complex or kNN edges from tissue x/y; nodes carry log-normalised expression of 3,000 HVGs) encoded by a two-layer GCN trained with Deep Graph Infomax (contrast against node-permuted graphs) plus a spatial regularisation loss that pushes apart embeddings of cells that are far apart in Euclidean tissue distance (strength gamma = 0.1 by default) (Methods "Spatially regularized Deep Graph Infomax").
- Outputs: Leiden domains on the embedding, and a "pseudo-Spatiotemporal Map" (pSM) = diffusion pseudotime on the embedding, rooted by default at the cell with the largest summed embedding distance (Methods "Domain segmentation and pseudo-Spatiotemporal Map").
- Validation: domain ARI on 12 DLPFC Visium sections (median 0.427, second to BayesSpace 0.438) (Fig. 2a); qualitative pSM comparisons with Seurat, Monocle, Slingshot and stLearn on DLPFC, Stereo-seq olfactory bulb, chicken heart (pSM computed separately per time point because pseudotime "across tissues with different time points may not be comparable"), and breast cancer (Figs. 3-5).
- Genes: domain markers by Wilcoxon with BH (Scanpy); "genes exhibiting the highest correlation between expression and the pSM" ranked without a test (Fig. 3c); GO by one-sided Fisher tests without multiple-testing correction (Fig. 2g legend); space-constrained CellChat.
- Replicates: none; each section analysed alone.
- Plot types: domain maps vs annotation; UMAPs coloured by layer and by distance to an origin; pSM maps on tissue; pSM vs UMAP-1 scatter; dot plots of domain markers; GO bars; CellChat maps.
- Software: Python package SpaceFlow (PyTorch, PyTorch Geometric), https://github.com/hongleir/SpaceFlow; Zenodo 6668286.
- Limitations (authors): Euclidean spatial regularisation assumes "nearby cells with similar gene expression are more closely related"; may not fit disjoint or geometrically complex tissues; strong regularisation over-smooths (Discussion; Methods).
