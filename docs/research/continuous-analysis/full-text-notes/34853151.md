## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Design: female C57BL/6J mice, bilateral renal pedicle clamp 34 min (titrated to match 22 min in males by BUN, creatinine, transdermal GFR and qPCR of Havcr1/Lcn2; Fig. 1). Visium (55 um spots) of coronal kidney sections at sham, 4 h, 12 h, 2 days and 6 weeks (Methods "Bilateral IRI"; Fig. 1A). About 1,500 spots per library; 16,856 genes over the time course. The text describes one library per time point integrated with Space Ranger "agg"; no replicate Visium sections per time point are described, and the Discussion defers "biologic replicate variability attributed to tissue slice variation" to future work. Kidney-function and qPCR measurements used n = 3-6 mice per time point.
- Model / statistic: descriptive. Seurat 4 (SCTransform, merge, DEGs via Seurat functions, GO by ToppFun); Giotto with PAGE enrichment of snRNA-seq cell-type signatures (from Kirita 2020, male) to label spots; Leiden clustering; SPOTlight deconvolution with 26 cell types (including injured PT "InjPT" and failed-repair PT "FR-PTC") and co-occurrence "interaction graphs" per time point (Fig. 4). Statistics only for function/qPCR (t tests, ANOVA).
- Key findings: many PT genes fall rapidly after injury and recover with repair (Aadat, a PTs3 DEG); Lcn2 rises fast in medulla and papilla, less in cortex; Cryab falls in papilla but rises in S3 with injury; Cfh rises everywhere at 6 weeks (Fig. 3C). Co-occurrence of injured PT with T cells and macrophages increases and persists to 6 weeks (Fig. 4B-C), with F4/80 around Kim-1+ tubules at 6 weeks (Fig. 4D). Regions are discrete: cortex, outer medulla, papilla, and spot cell types (PTs1-2, PTs3).
- Null / calibration: none for the spatial data (no replicate-based tests on Visium).
- Validation: immunofluorescence (LTL, AQP2, nephrin; F4/80 and Kim-1); comparison of female sham to a published male sham Visium sample (Melo Ferreira 2021).
- Plots: spatial feature maps per time point; Giotto PAGE enrichment maps; SPOTlight scatterpies by region; cell-type interaction graphs per time point; an interactive web viewer (humphreyslab.com/SingleCell).
- Software: Space Ranger 1.2.1, Seurat 4.0.3, Giotto (PAGE), SPOTlight, ToppFun, GraphPad Prism. No Monocle2.
- Limitations stated: 55 um spots of 10-50 cells; cell-type markers from a male snRNA-seq atlas; future need to handle replicate and slice variability. Limitations for OUR design: female mice (ours are male); one section per time point; regional and cell-type (discrete) resolution only.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
A descriptive Visium IR time course (female, one section per time point) with regional and cell-type resolution. It supports the gap claim (no continuous PT-position model of AKI) and offers time-resolved candidate markers (Aadat loss in PT S3, Lcn2 medullary induction, Cryab S3 induction) but no statistical method or replicate design to borrow.
