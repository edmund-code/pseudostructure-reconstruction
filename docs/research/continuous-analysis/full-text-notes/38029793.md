## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Data: snRNA-seq of developing and adult cerebellum, about 396,000 nuclei, human, mouse and opossum, 9-12 stages each; at least 2 biological replicates per stage except human 20 wpc (Supp. Methods "Data reporting"). Validation by multiplexed smFISH in one 12-wpc human cerebellum and mouse in situ atlases.
- Stage correspondences (Supp. Methods "Stage correspondences across species"; Extended Data Fig. 2e-g): three distance matrices between stages, each aligned by DTW with mouse as focal species: (I) Spearman correlation distance of stage pseudobulks on shared overdispersed orthologues; (II) Manhattan distance of pseudoage distributions; (III) Manhattan distance of cell-state proportions. Ties resolved by the transcriptome distance. The authors note correspondences depend on sampling and "should not be interpreted as absolute best matches".
- Shared pseudotime (Supp. Methods "Pseudotemporal ordering"): Purkinje and granule-cell lineage cells of all three species, three-way 1:1 orthologues, Harmony on 50 PCs with library and species as covariates, Scanpy diffusion pseudotime from a chosen root (Fig. 3d). Checked by matching cell states having comparable pseudotime (Extended Data Fig. 9b-c).
- Gene trajectories (Supp. Methods "Gene expression trajectories"): pseudotime split into 10 equal bins; per bin, pseudobulk per biological replicate (>= 50 cells), then the MEAN across replicates per species. Dynamic genes = overdispersed on the binned counts (variance-to-mean ratio above a Poisson expectation, alpha = 1), per species, intersected across species. CPM; each orthologue entered once per species; Mfuzz soft clustering into 8 clusters; centre of mass per trajectory; clusters ordered by mean centre of mass of confident members (membership > 0.5) (Fig. 3f, Extended Data Fig. 9f-g).
- Conservation tiers: a "similarity P-value" is computed from the two orthologues' cluster-membership vectors (formula in the PDF image), BH-adjusted. Strongly preserved = all orthologues confidently in the same cluster (membership > 0.5) and P > 0.5; diverged = differential assignment with adjusted P < 0.05 for at least one pair; intermediate = the rest; genes with any orthologue's max membership <= 0.5 are excluded. On average 23% preserved, 17% diverged, 60% intermediate (Fig. 3g). DTW distances between orthologue trajectories corroborate the tiers (Extended Data Fig. 9h); max and min pairwise DTW distances rank lineage-specific changes (Fig. 4c). Opossum as outgroup polarises changes to the human or mouse lineage (Fig. 4a-b).
- Null / calibration: no permutation null for the tiers; the P value comes from membership scores, not from replicates or label shuffling. Replicate variation is averaged away before clustering. Composition differences use a Bayesian hierarchical binomial model with replicate-level variation (RStan; 95% HDI) (Extended Data Fig. 7d). Presence/absence calls use within-species relative expression (gene's maximum in a cell type vs its maximum across cell types, >= 30%), a 50-CPM floor in >= 2 pseudobulks, >= 5-fold differences, and removal of orthologues with > 3-fold exon-length differences (Supp. Methods "Presence/absence expression differences").
- Plots: trajectory-cluster panels ordered by centre of mass with preserved orthologues highlighted (Fig. 3f); stacked proportions of preserved/intermediate/diverged in early, mid, late clusters (Fig. 3g); alluvial plots of each orthologue's cluster in each species (ggalluvial; Fig. 4a, Extended Data Fig. 10b); min vs max pairwise DTW distance scatter (Fig. 4c); centre-of-mass shift distributions by tier (Extended Data Fig. 10a); DTW stage-correspondence matrices (Extended Data Fig. 2e-g).
- Software: custom code at https://gitlab.com/kaessmannlab/mammalian-cerebellum (R/Python; LIGER, Harmony, Scanpy, Mfuzz, dtw, ggalluvial, RStan).
- Limitations stated: stage correspondences depend on sampling; cross-species correlation comparisons limited to shared HVGs and imperfect state matching; human fragments may misrepresent proportions; lineage-specific genes and isoforms not evaluated. Limitations for OUR design: tiers are computed on replicate means with an analytic membership-based P; nothing tells whether a "diverged" call exceeds individual-to-individual variation; with one human donor, donor and species are confounded.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Good descriptive template for human vs mouse PT: shared axis, binned pseudobulk curves, soft clusters ordered by centre of mass, alluvial cluster-switch plots, and within-species relative expression to avoid cross-species level artefacts (useful given our probe panels). Its tier statistics ignore replicate variation, so pair them with specimen-level relabeling; with one human donor, call "diverged" genes candidates only.
