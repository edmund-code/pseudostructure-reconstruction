## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Coordinate: TissueTag annotates H&E (pixel random forest trained on AIRE-high = medulla, ARPP21-high = cortex spots, then manual correction) into cortex, medulla, edge (capsule + septa), Hassall's corpuscles, PVS, artefacts. OrganAxis = a signed, normalised, sigmoid-shaped function H of the distances from a point to two landmarks (engineered to be sensitive near the boundary). The thymus Cortico-Medullary Axis is CMA = 0.2 x H(edge-to-cortex) + 0.8 x H(cortex-to-medulla), computed on a 15-um hexagonal grid (k = 10 neighbours) and transferred to Visium spots or IBEX nuclei (Methods "Formulation of the CMA"). Described as "scale- and rotation-invariant" and built from landmarks alone, so it applies to any 2-D/3-D modality.
- Binning: ten levels (capsular, subcapsular, cortical 1-3, cortical CMJ, medullary CMJ, medullary 1-3) with the same cut-offs for all samples (Results "T lineage trajectories"; Methods).
- Validation of the axis: Spearman correlation of the first 10 PCs with CMA, weighted by cumulative explained variance, vs the same for genes-per-spot (Fig. 2f); no association with age or donor (Ext. Data Fig. 3d).
- Group comparison (fetal vs paediatric), only for 65 CellphoneDB cytokines/chemokines (Fig. 3f; Methods "Cytokine two-way ANOVA analysis and cosine similarity"): log-normalised Visium expression averaged per SAMPLE and CMA bin (one point per sample per bin; n = 12 fetal and n = 16 paediatric samples, i.e. sections, several per donor); two-way ANOVA (statsmodels anova_lm, type 2) with age group, CMA bin and their interaction; Bonferroni; cosine similarity between the fetal and paediatric profiles of the per-bin medians across samples (uncentred). Result: most cytokines differ in level between ages, but most have a non-significant interaction and "very high cosine similarity"; exceptions (IL34, IL33, IL1R1, CCL2, SPP1) shift from fetal (sub)capsule to paediatric medulla. Hierarchical (Ward) clustering of fetal binned profiles.
- Other analyses: cell2location deconvolution mapped to CMA bins; weighted mean CMA position of cells and genes (dot product of abundance and CMA / sum); second axis = linear distance to nearest Hassall's corpuscle (25-um bins, 350-um cut-off), with a 2-D plot of mean CMA vs mean HC distance (Fig. 5d,f); CITE-seq pseudotime (Slingshot) mapped to CMA (median and 0.05-0.95 quantiles per substage, Fig. 6d-f); scFates on mTECII/III.
- Replicates: 29 donors in the single-cell atlas; Visium fetal 12 samples from 7 donors, paediatric 16 samples from 6 donors; IBEX 8 donors. The ANOVA treats samples (sections) as independent and bins within a sample as independent observations; no donor or sample random effect.
- Plot types: H&E with continuous vs binned CMA overlays (Figs 2c, 3b); UMAPs coloured by CMA; per-bin dot plots of cell-type abundance (Figs 3e, 4c, 6a); cytokine heat map with cosine bars and interaction dots (Fig. 3f); per-bin boxplots across samples with cosine annotated (Ext. Data Fig. 4b); 2-D weighted-mean scatter (Fig. 5d,f); smoothed mean +/- s.e.m. lines along pseudotime (Fig. 6g, Ext. Data Fig. 8b, not along CMA).
- Software: Python; TissueTag (github.com/Teichlab/TissueTag, github.com/nadavyayon/TissueTag); analysis code github.com/Teichlab/thymus_spatial_atlas; tutorial organ-axis-tutorial.readthedocs.io.
- Limitations (authors): lobules with little cortex or medulla excluded; IBEX antibody issues. Limitations for us: the CMA is built from H&E landmarks in tissue x/y (our method excludes this for reconstruction); the group test is section-level ANOVA without a repeated-measures or donor term; cosine similarity of uncentred positive profiles is close to 1 for most genes and is not offset-invariant on the log scale.

## Fit to our design
Medium for figures and the comparison layout (fixed bins, a shape-similarity bar next to an interaction test per gene or pathway, weighted mean position per gene). Low for the coordinate (H&E landmarks in tissue x/y) and for the inference (section-level ANOVA). With 2 vs 2 specimens, the interaction must be specimen-level and calibrated.
