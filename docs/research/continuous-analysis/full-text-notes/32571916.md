## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Design: snRNA-seq (10x v2, nuclei, zUMIs with exonic + intronic reads) of male C57BL/6J mouse kidneys after bilateral
  ischaemia-reperfusion (18 min clamp), at 4 h, 12 h, 2 d, 14 d and 6 wk, plus sham/control; n = 3 mice per group
  (Fig. 1A). 26,643 healthy nuclei; 99,935 AKI nuclei integrated with Harmony (Results; Fig. 1C-D).
- PT analysis: PT nuclei re-clustered WITHOUT Harmony into healthy S1, S2, S3, "injured S1/2", "injured S3",
  "severe injured PT", "repairing PT" and "failed-repair PT" (FR-PTC; Vcam1, Dcdc2a, Sema5a; loss of Slc5a12,
  Slc22a30, Slc7a13) (Results; Fig. 2A). Injured S1/2 and S3 express Havcr1 and Myc and are mostly 4 h and 12 h nuclei;
  severe injured PT adds Krt20 and heat-shock genes; repairing PT (2 d) has Top2a and cell-cycle terms; FR-PTC reaches
  nearly 30% of PT at 14 d and ~8% at 6 wk (Fig. 2B).
- Pseudotime: Monocle2 on the PT nuclei using the union of HVGs; "The mouse trajectory began with injury, and most cells
  progressed to healthy S1/S2 or S2/S3 proximal tubule segments, but FR-PTCs formed an alternate branch" (Fig. 2E).
  BEAM gives branch-dependent genes, clustered by pseudotime pattern and annotated by GO (Fig. 2F; Methods say two
  clusters, the legend says three). SCENIC regulons along the same trajectory (Fig. 3).
- Statistics / replicates: three mice per time point, but no test treats mice as replicates. DE and GSEA are cluster
  level (Seurat FindAllMarkers); BEAM tests are on nuclei. The only replicate-level statistics are one-way ANOVA with
  Dunnett's test on deconvolved fractions of an external bulk RNA-seq IRI time course (Fig. 2D).
- Validation: immunofluorescence for VCAM1/Kim1/LTL (Fig. 2C), alphaSMA/PDGFRb (Fig. 4D), F4/80 (SI); BSEQ-sc
  deconvolution of external bulk data (mouse IRI 1-year course, folic acid model, human allograft protocol biopsies,
  ageing rat kidney); Human Protein Atlas images of scattered VCAM1+/DCDC2+ cells in healthy human kidney.
- Location along PT: segment identity is carried only by discrete labels (injured S1/2 vs injured S3). FR-PTCs are
  described as scattered: VCAM1+ FR-PTCs "emerged within Kim1-positive injured tubule in a scattered manner" and FR-PTC
  marker cells in healthy human kidney are "scattered ... throughout the proximal tubule" (Results). No continuous
  position along S1->S3 is estimated.
- Plots: UMAPs; dot plots of cluster markers; stacked bars of cluster composition by time point; Monocle2 tree coloured
  by cluster; gene-cluster pseudotime curves for successful vs failed repair with GO terms (Fig. 2F); regulon heat map
  and regulon pseudotime curves (Fig. 3); ligand-receptor heat maps over time (Figs 5-6).
- Software: Seurat v3, Harmony, SoupX, Scrublet, zUMIs/STAR, Monocle2 (BEAM), SCENIC/GRNBoost2, BSEQ-sc. Data GEO
  GSE139107.
- Limitations stated: none specific; open questions only (Discussion).
- Limitations for our design: nuclei, not tissue sections; the pseudotime is an injury-repair axis whose two terminal
  arms are segment groups (S1/S2 vs S2/S3), so anatomical position and injury state are mixed in one coordinate; mice
  are not the unit of any test along the trajectory.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Biological reference, not a methods template. It supplies injury-state marker sets, a time course to place our IR
mice against (state the IR time point when citing), and two location claims we can test on a continuous axis:
acute injury split into S1/2 vs S3 states, and failed-repair cells scattered along the PT. Our continuous position x
injury model would be new relative to it.
