## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model: three latent GP functions per gene (trunk and two branches) with the same squared-exponential kernel, constrained to intersect at the branching time t_b (a modified version of the DEtime crossing kernel, allowing a kink at t_b). Gaussian likelihood with i.i.d. noise. Each cell's branch membership is a latent indicator, inferred per gene by mean-field variational inference with a prior from the global branching labels (0.99 after the global branch point; uninformative before it). Sparse inducing points (e.g. 30) for scale; GPflow.
- Branching time: posterior over a grid of candidate t_b from the approximate marginal likelihood (eq. 2); credible region from that posterior. Evidence for branching: log Bayes factor of branching vs not branching (t_b -> infinity), integrating over t_b (eq. 3).
- Ordering: P(gene A branches before t) and P(A branches before B) from posterior samples (eqs 4-5); a directed "branch order network" with edges at 95% posterior probability (Figs 7, 11); posterior rank intervals.
- Null / calibration: no P values or FDR; genes ranked by Bayes factor (log BF > 0 called "evidence of branching").
- Replicates: none; cells are observations; no sample hierarchy.
- Validation: synthetic (150 cells, 40 genes, early/late/no-branch groups, six noise levels): AUC for detecting branching similar to or better than BEAM, better than MFA (Table 3); branching-time RMSE lower than BEAM in every setting (Table 4); BEAM estimates biased toward the global branching time and its last-crossing rule fails for transitory genes (Fig. 3; APOE example, Fig. 6). Real data: haematopoiesis (Wishbone pseudotime; 1,072 pre-filtered genes, 839 with log BF > 0, subsampled to 467 cells) and droplet mESC (DPT pseudotime; 998 genes, 337 branching, 335 cells).
- Plots: gene fit with cell-assignment probabilities and the branching-time posterior beneath (Figs 1, 6, 8, 10); all genes' most likely branching times with credible regions ordered along pseudotime (Figs 5a, 9a); marker-gene branching intervals (Fig. 5b,c); branch-order networks (Figs 7, 11).
- Software: Python, github.com/ManchesterBioinference/BranchedGP (Apache 2.0), GPflow.
- Limitations stated: per-gene cell assignments can be inconsistent across genes (hence the strong prior); Gaussian likelihood (non-Gaussian extension planned); pseudotime treated as fixed; single branching point. Limitations for us: one-sided (shared trunk before t_b); units-as-replicates; Gaussian noise.

Replacement: "BGP's per-gene branching time is an analogue of a divergence position, but it assumes identical curves before divergence: across species this needs level-free curves first; for IR vs control (same panel) it applies directly. ## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
For IR vs control on the same panel, a per-gene divergence position with a credible region and a P(A diverges before B) network are attractive "where" summaries, but DEtime (labels known) fits better than BGP (labels inferred). Both are Gaussian, one-sided and cell-level; specimen spread must come from per-specimen fits or relabeling. For species, only after removing the panel offset.
