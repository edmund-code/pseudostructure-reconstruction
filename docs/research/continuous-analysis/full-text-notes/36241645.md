## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic: per dataset, PCA on HVGs, kNN graph, Leiden clusters, cluster centroids (median PCs), minimum spanning tree over centroids = trajectory tree. The two trees are aligned with a dynamic-programming unordered-tree alignment (bounded degree), cost = 1 - Spearman correlation between centroid expression, gap penalty delta = 1 (Methods eqs 1-8). Then, for a chosen pair of aligned root-to-leaf paths, cells are ordered by diffusion pseudotime and aligned with DTW on a gene set (intersection of HVGs) (Fig. 1b; Methods "Pseudotime ordering", "Dynamic time warping").
- Gene-kinetics comparison (Methods "Analysis of molecular patterns"): per aligned path, select HVGs, compute DTW similarity per gene, then split genes by the sign of linear-regression slopes (both up; up in one and down in the other) and filter by set difference. Ontology enrichment of the "opposite" groups with Metascape (Fig. 6e).
- Null / calibration: none for alignments or gene kinetics. The only P values are one-sided Wilcoxon tests comparing benchmark scores between methods (Fig. 4a) and Metascape hypergeometric enrichment.
- Replicates: none. One dataset per side (e.g. Velten et al. individual 1, 915 cells after filtering, vs Paul et al. mouse, 2,671 cells). MAGIC denoising was applied to every real dataset.
- Validation: dyngen simulations (68 datasets, split into pairs) scoring normalized alignment distance and an "average trajectory conservation" (ATC) score; CAPITAL beat merging with Scanorama, scVI and Seurat on ATC (Fig. 4). Real: human-human bone marrow (Setty vs Velten; Fig. 5) and human-mouse bone marrow (Velten vs Paul; Fig. 6), with conserved marker heatmaps and examples of differing kinetics (IRF7, CSF1 higher in human; ELANE higher in mouse).
- Plots: UMAP with cluster-tree overlay; aligned tree diagram with "#" gaps (Fig. 5c, 6b); cell-cell DTW matching lines (Fig. 5d); pseudotime-aligned marker kinetics (Fig. 5e); marker heatmaps ordered by pseudotime per species (Fig. 6c); ontology heatmaps for opposite-kinetics genes (Fig. 6e).
- Software: Python, built on Scanpy; https://github.com/ykat0/capital; Code Ocean capsule.
- Limitations stated: MST trajectory may not be the best; accuracy degrades when global topologies differ; trees only (no cycles or disconnected graphs). The authors note the human-mouse differences may "result from technical issues such as cell cycle effects". Limitations for OUR design: built for branching trees; no statistics on gene differences; single dataset per species; level differences are not separated from shape.
