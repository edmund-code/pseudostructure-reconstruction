## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Pseudotime ("Waterfall 2"): PCA of single-cell TPM; k-means centres; a minimum spanning tree connecting the centres; each cell projected perpendicularly onto the nearest MST segment or vertex; segments straightened into one line; pseudotime scaled 0-1; origin and direction set with known markers (Sox11, Tbr2, Blbp, Gfap). A branch cluster (SA) is set aside. The authors report the reconstructed path is shorter than random orderings (details in Supplementary Methods).
- Gene analysis ("Waterfall 3"): pseudotime split into 40 bins (~2.5 cells each); mean expression per bin; an HMM (Baum-Welch, then Viterbi) gives a binary on/high vs off/low state per bin for each gene.
- Gene selection: Spearman correlation of TPM with pseudotime; top 1,000 positive (UP1000, rho > 0.20) and top 1,000 negative (DOWN1000, rho < -0.13). These are rank cut-offs, not significance tests.
- Pathways: GO/KEGG/WikiPathways enrichment of UP1000 and DOWN1000 (hypergeometric, Holm-Bonferroni; Fig. 5B). Pathway timing: "On/off binary states of expressed genes within each functional entity were averaged and normalized to show the timing of the transition" (Fig. 6A,C). Cell-cycle checkpoint sets switch on in cell-cycle order (G1/S, then G2/M, then segregation), and ribosomal subunits switch on before any checkpoint set (Fig. 6C, Fig. S6D). Alternative analysis: the proportion of positively vs negatively correlated genes in each WikiPathways entity calls a pathway "activated" or "inactivated" (Fig. S7).
- Null / calibration: none for genes (rank cut-offs); hypergeometric tests for enrichment. Cells are the observations.
- Replicates: none modelled (142 CFP+ cells from Nestin-CFPnuc mice; batches checked for consistency only).
- Validation: known markers in expected order; immunohistology of Aldoc and Stmn1 (n = 3 animals); Hopx-CreERT2 lineage tracing; electrophysiology.
- Plots: binary on/off "molecular cascade" heat map of the top 150 UP and DOWN genes sorted by transition point, with a cell-density histogram on top (Fig. 4B); marker profiles with loess and 95% CI and HMM states below (Fig. 2D); averaged binary-state curves per functional entity (Fig. 6A,C); schematic of sequential waves (Fig. 7).
- Software: custom R code in the supplementary data.
- Limitations for us: binarisation of 2.5-cell bins discards graded signal; rank-based gene lists without error control; cells as replicates.

- a-gene-dynamics.md:57 - "The named precedent for cascade/waterfall figures. We would draw it from fitted curves, not binarised data." - VERDICT: correct but misses an important point. Waterfall also times pathways: it averages members' on/off states per functional entity and shows that cell-cycle checkpoint sets switch on in the known cell-cycle order and ribosomal genes switch on first (Fig. 6). That is an early precedent for "pathway order along the axis matches known biological order", alongside the liver enzyme-order test. Suggested addition: "It also times pathways by averaging members' on/off states per functional set (Fig. 6) and checks that cell-cycle checkpoint sets switch on in cell-cycle order."

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
Figure precedent only, plus a pathway-timing precedent. Its per-set average of binary states is a crude version of our median program curve (it also cancels opposite-direction members); its direction-split alternative (share of up vs down members per set, Fig. S7) is a cheap heterogeneity check we could report beside program curves.
