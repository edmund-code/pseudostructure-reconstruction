## What the paper actually does (from the abstract only)
- Measured promoter activity of about 100 E. coli genes in parallel in living cells with GFP and Lux reporter libraries, minute resolution, about 10% accuracy.
- In amino-acid biosynthesis systems, found a temporal expression program and an expression hierarchy that matches enzyme order in unbranched pathways: the closer an enzyme is to the start of the pathway, the shorter the response time of its promoter activation AND the higher its maximal promoter activity.
- Mathematical analysis suggests this "just-in-time" program is optimal for reaching a production goal quickly with minimal total enzyme production.
- No claim beyond the abstract can be made about methods, statistics, number of pathways or figures.
