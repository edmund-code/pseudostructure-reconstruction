## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic. A benchmark of trajectory-inference (TI) tools, not a statistical method. Every method's output
  is converted into one "common probabilistic trajectory model": a milestone network, per-cell milestone
  percentages, and regions of delayed commitment (preprint Methods "Common trajectory model", Supp Fig 16; Rmd
  "Common trajectory model", 7 wrapper types).
  - Preprint v1: 29 of 59 listed methods evaluated (Abstract; Methods "Method selection"; Table 1), three metrics:
    correlation of geodesic cell-cell distances, random-forest MSE of neighbourhoods, edge-flip topology score
    (Fig 1c; Methods "Evaluation metrics"). Default parameters only.
  - Journal version: "45 of these methods on 110 real and 229 synthetic datasets for cellular ordering, topology,
    scalability and usability" (PubMed abstract). Four accuracy metrics: Hamming-Ipsen-Mikhailov (topology),
    F1 between branch assignments, Spearman correlation of geodesic distances (cell positions), and featureimp_wcor
    (agreement of trajectory-associated genes via random-forest feature importance) (Rmd Fig 1 caption; Online
    Methods "Benchmark metrics"). Plus scalability (shape-constrained additive models of time/memory against
    log10 cells and features), stability (10 subsamples of 95% cells and 95% features), and a usability checklist.
    Off-the-shelf baselines were added: PCA component 1, angle (cyclic), and PCA+mclust+MST (Rmd "Off-the-shelf
    methods").
- Null / calibration / significance. No P values on the ranking. Scores are normalised per dataset (preprint:
  percentile ranks then harmonic mean over metrics; journal Rmd: per-dataset standardisation mapped back to [0,1],
  arithmetic means within source and trajectory type, then geometric mean over metrics; Rmd "Score aggregation").
  The preprint tests the effect of priors and of algorithm components with two-tailed Mann-Whitney U tests and BH
  correction (preprint Methods "Effect of prior information", "Effect of algorithm components"). Replicate unit =
  dataset (plus repeated runs).
- Replicates / multiple samples. Not applicable: each dataset is one trajectory; no between-condition or
  multi-sample comparison anywhere in either version.
- Validation. Real datasets with gold standards (FACS, time, sample origin) or silver standards (author clustering);
  synthetic data from dyngen (preprint) and, in the journal version, also dyntoy, PROSSTT and Splatter (Rmd
  "Synthetic datasets"). Preprint: real-vs-synthetic score correlation 0.7-0.9 (Results; Supp Fig 6). Journal Rmd:
  sources correlate 0.5-0.9 with gold-standard real datasets.
- Main findings. Preprint: Slingshot, TSCAN and Monocle DDRTree top overall; SCORPIUS best on linear data; topology
  rarely recovered exactly; methods with principal curves, k-means or graph building tended to score slightly higher
  (adjusted P 0.088, not significant; Supp Table 2). Journal Rmd: "no one-size-fits-all" method; PAGA, Slingshot and
  SCORPIUS good across the board; one method gives a top model on about 27% of datasets, rising to 74% with 6 more
  methods (complementarity figure); most graph/tree methods scale poorly.
- Advice. Choose by expected topology and dataset dimensions (guidelines app guidelines.dynverse.org); confirm a
  trajectory "and the downstream results and/or hypotheses originating from it" with multiple TI methods; also try
  methods with fewer topology assumptions (preprint "Practical guidelines"; Rmd Discussion).
- Plot types. Summary "funky heatmap" of methods x metrics/sources/trajectory types/time/QC (preprint Fig 2; Rmd
  results_summary/results_detailed); predicted-vs-true topology heatmaps (preprint Fig 3); QC checklist chart
  (preprint Fig 4); guideline flowchart (preprint Fig 5; Rmd user_guidelines); complementarity curves and example
  predictions on a shared MDS (Rmd).
- Software. R; dynverse packages dynwrap, dyneval, dynguidelines, dynplot, method containers (dynmethods,
  docker/singularity); benchmark.dynverse.org; data on Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.1443566 (Rmd "Data and code
  availability"). The name "dyno" does not appear in either text.
- Limitations the authors state. Default parameters only (preprint); the common model cannot carry uncertainty of
  a cell's position (preprint Discussion; Rmd calls for uncertainty in a future common interface); selection bias
  in included tools (Rmd Usability); synthetic data may favour methods whose model matches the simulator.
- Limitations for OUR design. Benchmarks cells from dissociated scRNA-seq, not spatial aggregates; scores
  ordering and topology only. Its "trajectory DE features" metric (featureimp_wcor) checks whether the same genes
  look trajectory-associated, not whether any between-condition test is calibrated. It says nothing about
  replicate-aware or between-condition statistics.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Useful only as background on the axis itself: principal-curve methods (SCORPIUS, Slingshot) did well on linear
data, consistent with our elastic principal curve, and the authors' advice to confirm a trajectory and its
downstream results with several TI methods supports keeping DPT/scFates as comparators. It gives no guidance on
replicate-aware or between-condition curve statistics.
