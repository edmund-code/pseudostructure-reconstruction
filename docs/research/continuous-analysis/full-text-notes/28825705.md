## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model: Monocle 2 learns a principal tree by reversed graph embedding. Default DDRTree: a linear orthogonal projection W from gene space to a latent space, soft k-means of the latent points onto K centroids, and a spanning tree on the centroids, optimised jointly (Online Methods eq. with lambda, gamma, sigma; K chosen by a formula in N). Cells are projected onto the nearest tree segment; pseudotime = geodesic distance from a user-chosen root tip; segments ("States") are assigned by depth-first traversal. Feature genes are chosen by "dpFeature" (tSNE + density-peak clustering + DE between clusters).
- Gene tests: none new; it uses BEAM from the Census paper (887 branch-dependent genes in myoblasts at FDR < 10%; Olsson data FDR < 1%).
- Null / calibration: not applicable (trajectory method). Accuracy = Pearson/Kendall correlation with a marker-based reference ordering and ARI for branch assignment; robustness = 25 subsamples of 80% of cells, pairwise comparison; parameter sweeps (Fig. 1D,E; Supplementary Figs 9-14).
- Replicates: none; genetic knockouts (Gfi1, Irf8) projected onto one trajectory to show "diversion" to alternative fates (Fig. 2).
- Validation: simulated differentiation (SDE network, up to 5 branches), Paul et al. and Olsson et al. myeloid data, myoblasts; comparison with Monocle 1, Wishbone, DPT, SLICER.
- Plots: tree layouts coloured by cell type or genotype (Fig. 2); multi-branch heatmaps and kinetic curves (Methods).
- Software: R `monocle` (Monocle 2), `DDRTree` on CRAN; GitHub cole-trapnell-lab/monocle-release and monocle2-rge-paper.
- Limitations for us: a trajectory-inference method; our coordinate already comes from an elastic principal curve (a related principal-graph idea). Nothing here on gene dynamics or replicates.
