## What the paper actually does (from the main text)
- Biology paper, not a methods paper: Smart-seq2-type scRNA-seq of 4,645 cells from 19 melanomas; malignant cells called by inferred CNVs (averaging expression over 100-gene windows); t-SNE + DBSCAN clustering of non-malignant cells; PCA of malignant cells (PC1 correlated with the number of genes detected per cell, "likely reflects technical aspects"); cell-cycle G1/S and G2/M signatures to call cycling cells; MITF-high and AXL-high programs defined by correlation with MITF; bulk deconvolution of 471 TCGA melanomas with single-cell-derived cell-type signatures; CAF-T cell interaction genes; T-cell exhaustion programs.
- Gene-set scores are used throughout ("relative expression" of signatures, Fig. 2A, 3A-D, 5D), but how they are computed (including any control gene sets matched on expression) is in the supplement, which we could not read.
- Statistics: binomial and t-tests for paired pre/post-treatment shifts (Fig. 3D), permutation test for exhaustion genes (Fig. 5E-F), hypergeometric tests; cells used as units in most single-cell comparisons.
- Software: none released in the paper; data GSE72056, GSE77940.

## Review check
- continuous-analysis/b-continuous-pathways.md:26 (row 9, item ii) - "Tirosh 2016 (PMID 27124452; control-set method as implemented in Seurat AddModuleScore ...)" and "(ii) The Tirosh/Seurat score subtracts control genes drawn from the same expression bins, which removes drift in depth and expression level." - VERDICT: unsupported by the full text we could read. The main text does not describe the control-gene method; the description rests on the Seurat documentation, as the review's own verification note (b:134) says. "Removes drift in depth" is the review's interpretation, not something the main text or the Seurat page states. It also misses the known side effect (UCell, PMID 34285779, Fig. 1D): because the control bins come from dataset-wide averages, scores depend on which cells are pooled.
  Replacement: "(ii) The Tirosh/Seurat score (as documented for Seurat AddModuleScore; the method is in Tirosh 2016's supplement, not read) subtracts the mean of control genes drawn from the same average-expression bins. The bins are computed over the whole data set, so scores shift with the pool of cells or units (UCell 2021, Fig. 1D)."
- b:134 (verification note) - "The Tirosh control-set score is described from the Seurat AddModuleScore documentation ... The method itself is in the paper's supplement, which we did not read." - VERDICT: correct and still true after this pass (supplement not accessible).

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the main text)
The paper is the origin citation for a per-cell signature score, not evidence about calibration. For us the relevant facts are the score's dependence on the expression bins of the pooled data (so compute per specimen) and that the main text itself treats the number of detected genes as a technical axis (PC1), which is the depth issue the review raises.
