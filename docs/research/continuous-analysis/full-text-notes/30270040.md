## What the paper actually does (from the preprint)
- Landmarks: laser-capture microdissection of 5 equal-length villus segments (8-10 villi > 500 um per mouse, 3 male mice), bulk RNA-seq (SMART-Seq v4), giving 62 villus-bottom and 43 villus-top landmark genes (Results; Methods "Laser capture microdissection").
- Coordinate: each enterocyte gets x_i = sum(top LM) / (sum(bottom LM) + sum(top LM)), with expression as a fraction of cellular UMIs. The same score is computed for the 5 LCM segments, and cells are binned into 6 zones V1-V6 by those cut-offs; crypt profile from the transit-amplifying clusters (Methods "Zonation reconstruction algorithm").
- Data: a public 10x scRNA-seq dataset of Lgr5-eGFP-negative cells (GEO GSM2644349, GSM2644350; Yan et al. 2017), Seurat 2.1; 1,383 enterocytes and TA cells after filtering. Replicate number was regressed out in Seurat preprocessing (Methods "scRNAseq analysis").
- Per-gene summary: zone means and SEM over cells; profiles max-normalised and sorted by centre of mass (Fig. 2a).
- Zonation test: the dynamic range (max - min of the mean-normalised V1-V6 profile) compared with 1,000 reshuffles of the cells' zone assignments; z-score, normal P, Storey q-values. 8,126 of 9,832 enterocyte genes (83%) zonated at q < 0.05 (Results; Methods). Cells are the units; there is no mouse-level test. The landmark genes are not stated to be excluded from the count.
- Programs: k-means (correlation distance, MATLAB) of max-normalised profiles of 2,118 highly expressed zonated genes into 5 clusters ordered bottom to top; hypergeometric GO enrichment per cluster with Storey q (Fig. 3a; Methods "Clustering and gene ontology enrichment"). GO-term mean profiles +- SEM are drawn per cluster.
- Biology: a Reg-gene antibacterial zone at the villus bottom (microbiome-dependent; germ-free smFISH, Fig. 3c-e); translation, splicing and mitochondrial genes decline towards the tip; amino-acid and carbohydrate transporters peak mid-villus, the peptide transporter Slc15a1 is shifted upward, and Npc1l1 plus lipoprotein machinery peak at the tips (Fig. 3f); a tip program with Egfr, Klf4, Fos, Junb and purine catabolism (Enpp3, Nt5e/CD73, Slc28a2, Ada), with Enpp3 rising from zone 4 and Nt5e only in zone 6 (Fig. 4). Heterogeneity "seems to be conserved" across duodenum, jejunum and ileum (Supp. Fig. 10, not read).
- Validation: smFISH profiles for 15 genes (each >= 10 villi from 3 mice; villus segmented into 20 units), Nt5e immunofluorescence (Fig. 2c, 4).
- Plot types: COM-sorted max-normalised zonation heatmap (Fig. 2a); scRNA-seq vs smFISH profile lines with SEM bands (Fig. 2c, 4a); cluster mean profiles with individual genes in grey and GO-term mean +- SEM (Fig. 3a); heatmaps and averaged profiles of nutrient transporter classes (Fig. 3f); tSNE coloured by zone (Fig. 1f).
- Software: custom MATLAB (reconstruction, k-means, smFISH dot counting), Seurat 2.1 (R), Kallisto/Sleuth for LCM RNA-seq; data GEO GSE109413. No package.
- Limitations for OUR design: the axis is a one-dimensional landmark ratio, binned into 6 zones; the test reshuffles cells (no specimen replication) and the coordinate shares genes with the tested set.

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the preprint)
Good as a framing and figure template (COM-sorted heatmap, cluster-then-GO with mean +- SEM profiles, smFISH-style validation), and as a precedent for anchoring a continuous expression score to physically defined segments. Weak as a statistical template: the zonation test reshuffles cells, the axis is binned into 6 zones, and 83% of genes are called, which shows that a "zonated or not" test on a strong axis calls almost everything; the useful outputs are position and shape, not significance.
