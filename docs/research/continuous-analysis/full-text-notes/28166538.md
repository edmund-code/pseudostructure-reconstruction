## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Coordinate: the porto-central axis is divided into nine layers (layer 1 = central vein, layer 9 = portal node). Six landmark genes (Glul, Cyp2e1, Ass1, Asl, Alb, Cyp2f2) measured by smFISH give layer-dependent expression distributions; a probabilistic algorithm gives each scRNA-seq hepatocyte a posterior over layers, with a geometric prior from hexagonal lobule layer areas (Extended Data Fig. 3). Accuracy saturates at six landmarks (ED Fig. 4). Validated by smFISH on 20 further genes (ED Fig. 5).
- Per gene: a reconstructed zonation profile over the nine layers (fraction of cellular UMI); "around 50%" zonated: 3,496 of 7,277 genes at q < 0.2 by Kruskal-Wallis. Profiles max-normalised and sorted by centre of mass in the heat map (Fig. 3c). Porto-central bias = (layer 1 - layer 9) / mean over layers (Methods, Statistical analysis); a separate PC/PP ratio (median layers 1-4 over median layers 6-9) is used for comparison with earlier studies (ED Fig. 7).
- Gene sets: mean zonation profiles of signalling-response gene sets with s.e.m. patches (Wnt up/down from APC-KO, hypoxia, Ha-ras, hypopituitary mice), compared by Wilcoxon rank-sum on porto-central bias (Fig. 3d-g; e.g. Wnt-activated n = 810 vs Wnt-inhibited n = 193, p < 1e-31).
- Pathways: KEGG hypergeometric enrichment of zonated genes, 25 of 186 pathways at q < 0.1 (Table S5); average max-normalised profiles of enriched pathways (ED Fig. 9a); periportal bias of secreted proteins, pericentral bias of detoxification genes (ED Fig. 9b,c).
- Non-monotonic class: concise set of 46 genes with Kruskal-Wallis q < 0.01 and a peak in layers 3-7 at least 10% above the maximum of layers 1 and 9 (Methods; ED Fig. 10a); examples Hamp, Igfbp2, Mup3, Cyp8b1; not explained by ploidy (ED Fig. 10e).
- Enzyme order (descriptive): the neutral bile-acid pathway Cyp7a1 -> Hsd3b7 -> Cyp8b1 -> Cyp27a1 shows "a spatial order that matched the position in the enzymatic cascade" (Fig. 4b, s.e.m. from 1,000 bootstrap iterations); likewise Igf1 (periportal) -> Igfbp2 (mid) -> Igfbp1 (pericentral). No test of order is reported.
- Spatially discordant enzyme pairs (Methods): from 62 KEGG metabolic maps, pairs sharing a substrate, sharing a product, or sequential (plus indirect E1 -> E3 pairs); both enzymes expressed and zonated (q < 0.2). Score S = [E1(x1) - E1(x2)] x [E2(x1) - E2(x2)], x_i = peak layer of enzyme i, profiles mean-normalised; negative = peaks at different layers. Null: 100,000 random pairs among the 731 expressed liver enzymes; pairs below the null's 10th percentile (-0.14) are reported (Table S6). The authors state that the direction of metabolite flow is hard to determine, so the table is a "resource". This scores discordance (division of labour), not agreement with reaction order.
- Null / replicates: per-gene Kruskal-Wallis across layers with cells as observations; scRNA-seq from four mice pooled; smFISH from at least 2 mice and 10 lobules per gene. No specimen-level test.
- Plots: COM-sorted heat map of max-normalised profiles (Fig. 3c); gene-set mean profiles with s.e.m. patches (Fig. 3d-g); enzyme profiles in cascade order (Fig. 4b); pathway mean profiles (ED Fig. 9a); non-monotonic set (ED Fig. 10a); bias-vs-bias scatter against earlier data (ED Fig. 7).
- Software: Matlab inference code "available upon request"; no package.
- Limitations for us: discrete layers from landmark genes (our coordinate is continuous from the transcriptome, so circularity matters more); cells pooled across mice.

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
The vocabulary (COM-sorted max-normalised heat maps, gene-set mean +/- s.e.m. curves, an end-ratio bias) transfers directly. The enzyme-pair discordance score is a ready template for division of labour inside PT pathways; an order test would be our own addition. Its statistics (cells pooled, Kruskal-Wallis across layers) are weaker than our specimen-level calibration.
