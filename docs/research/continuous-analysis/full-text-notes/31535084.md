## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Coordinate. Hepatocytes from 5 male mice are FACS-sorted into 8 gates using two inversely zonated surface markers,
  CD73 (pericentral) and E-cadherin (periportal), after size, viability, CD31/CD45-negative and tetraploid gating
  (Fig. 3a-b; Methods "Flow Cytometry and sorting"). Each marker-positive population is split into four equal-count
  sub-gates by intensity; gates 4 and 5 include cells below the positive threshold (mid-lobule). Gates are set per
  mouse. So gates are equal-count, marker-ranked bins, not physical layers; CIBERSORT on 17 scRNA-seq layer
  signatures estimates the layer mix of each gate (Methods "Comparing bulk mRNA with published scRNAseq", Fig. 3c).
- Assays per gate: bulk mRNA-seq (10,000 cells; 40 libraries, 2 failed), mass-spec proteomics (100,000 cells;
  3,210 proteins), miRNA microarrays (3 mice).
- Per-feature statistics.
  - Zonation call: Kruskal-Wallis across the 8 gates with the 5 mice as replicates per gate (n = 40, Methods
    "Computing zonation"), Benjamini-Hochberg FDR < 0.05 for proteins (1,672/3,051 = 55% zonated); miRNAs FDR <= 0.2
    (61/137). The KW test ignores that the 8 gates come from the same mouse.
  - Centre of mass (COM) over gates z = 1..8, a value in [1, 8] (Methods "Center of mass calculation"; the formula
    itself is an image not in the text). PC/PP split at COM 4.5 (Hnf4a KO analysis, APAP miRNA analysis).
  - Periportal bias = (mean of two periportal gates - mean of two pericentral gates) / mean over gates; used to
    compare mRNA and protein zonation (Fig. 5b; Spearman r = 0.39, all proteins).
- Set-level / regulatory statistics.
  - miR-122-5p targets: mean COM of 146 targets vs 1,000 random draws of 146 expressed genes, Wilcoxon rank-sum,
    p = 0.018 (Methods "Differential zonation of miR-122-5p targets"). This is a set-position test against random
    gene sets.
  - miRNA-target network: for each gene, Spearman correlation of its mRNA profile with the summed profile of its
    predicted regulating miRNAs, compared with 1,000 degree-preserving randomised networks (mfinder); empirical P,
    BH FDR <= 0.2 -> 11 genes (Fig. 7a; Methods "MiRNA-target network ...").
  - Hnf4a KO: Fisher exact test of up/down regulation x PC/PP (COM-based) class.
  - TCGA HCC: Spearman correlation of miRNA COM with tumour/normal log2 fold change.
- Replicates. 5 mice for mRNA and protein, 3 for miRNA; profiles shown as mean +- SEM across mice. Mice enter only as
  replicate observations in KW tests; no mixed model.
- Validation. Gate profiles agree with Halpern 2017 scRNA-seq reconstruction (Fig. 3c); immunofluorescence for
  selected proteins; qRT-PCR for 6 miRNAs (mean r = 0.70); NPC contamination checked with 9 cell-type signatures.
- Plots. Max-normalised heatmap of proteins sorted by COM (Fig. 5a); periportal-bias mRNA vs protein scatter (Fig. 5b);
  mRNA and protein line profiles +- SEM (Fig. 5c); proteomaps (Fig. 4a); mean expression vs COM scatter for miRNAs
  (Fig. 6a); miRNA-target profile pairs and network (Fig. 7).
- Software. No package; "Code ... available upon request" (Code availability).
- Limitations the authors state: gates mix several lobule layers; FACS cannot measure labile metabolites; proteomics
  misses low-abundance proteins.
- Limitations for OUR design: gates are equal-count, marker-ranked bins (like quantile bins), so COM is in bin
  units, not distance; tests treat mice as unpaired replicates; no shape model.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
A source for simple, level-free position summaries (COM, periportal bias) and for a set-position test (mean COM of a
gene set vs random sets of the same size). Its gates are equal-count marker bins, like our quantile bins, and its
tests treat mice as unpaired replicates, so take the summaries, not the inference.
