## What the paper actually does (abstract only, plus labelled public legends)
- From the abstract: spatial transcriptomics, spatial proteomics and smFISH build a spatial expression atlas of the adult human proximal small intestine; zonated expression and cell-type representation for epithelial, mesenchymal and immune cells along the crypt-villus axis; enterocytes switch from lipid droplet assembly and iron uptake at the villus bottom to chylomicron biosynthesis and iron release at the tip; villus tip cells are pro-immunogenic (recruiting gamma-delta T cells and macrophages), "in contrast to their immunosuppressive roles in mouse"; abundant serrated and branched villi at the tops of circular folds.
- From the public Extended Data legends only (not verified against the main text): 10x Visium n = 8 patients, CODEX n = 3, smFISH n = 8 (ED Fig. 1a); spots assigned to crypt and villus zones V1-V6 and to a stromal-epithelial axis; zonation profiles shown as the median across patients with MAD patches (ED Figs 4-5, 9e); max-normalised heat maps sorted by centre of mass (ED Fig. 10e); LCM RNA-seq (n = 8) and LCM proteomics (n = 3) of villus bottom vs top validate a "villus height bias" (Spearman R = 0.59 Visium vs LCM; R = 0.54 mRNA vs protein; ED Fig. 9); per-patient zonation profiles compared by clinical features with Kruskal-Wallis on correlation distances (ED Fig. 11); human-vs-mouse comparison of EEC hormone and immunomodulatory gene profiles (ED Figs 6a, 12c). Code: github.com/yotamharnik/ST_Human_SI; CODEX code github.com/tiroshlab/Spatial_small_intestine; data on Zenodo.
