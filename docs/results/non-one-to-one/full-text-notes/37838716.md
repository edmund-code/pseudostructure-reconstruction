# 37838716 - Song 2023, Nat Commun: Benchmarking strategies for cross-species integration of single-cell RNA sequencing data (BENGAL)
Read status: FULL TEXT READ (Europe PMC JATS, PMC10576752: main text, methods, figure and table legends). Supplementary
Information (Supplementary Figs. 1-43, Notes 1-4) not read.

## What the paper does
- 28 strategies: 4 homology mappings (Ensembl 106: one-to-one only "O2O"; plus one-to-many/many-to-many chosen by
  higher expression "HE" or stronger homology confidence "SH"; all genes for LIGER UINMF) × 9 integration algorithms,
  plus SAMap with tblastx maps (Methods). 16 tasks incl. human–mouse pancreas, human–macaque–pig hippocampus,
  frog–zebrafish embryo, 5-species heart (Table 1).
- Gene counts (Table 1): human–mouse pancreas used 11,248 one-to-one and 664 one-to-many/many-to-many genes.
- **Homology method mattered little:** "major differences were given by integration algorithms but not homology
  methods" (Results, Fig. 2a). In-paralogs raised scores only in the frog–zebrafish embryo task, where they are a large
  share of genes; "we did not observe significant improvements in benchmarking scores by adding in-paralogs in most
  tasks" (Results, "Impact of one-to-many and many-to-many homologous genes"; Supplementary Fig. 25). They changed HVG
  selection (Supplementary Fig. 26).
- **SAMap and continuous structure:** on the embryo task SAMap had "a relatively low trajectory conservation score";
  developmental stages were partly mixed, read as SAMap "prioritising identity-defining expression characteristics"
  (Results, "Integrating embryonic development"; Supplementary Fig. 19).
- SAMap's batch metrics are not computed (it outputs a graph); its alignment score is the highest in every task
  (Supplementary Fig. 17), which the authors note follows from SAMap operating directly on cross-species kNN edges.
- Guidelines (Discussion): close species → scVI/scANVI or Harmony; whole-body atlases or species without curated
  homology → SAMap; include in-paralogs when species share many one-to-many/many-to-many orthologs. SAMap is "a
  heuristic approach" that "does not provide explanations of the observed cell type expression similarity".
- Replicates: batches used as integration keys where balanced; no specimen-level inference.

## Fit to our question
At human–mouse distance the benchmark finds no general gain from adding in-paralogs, and SAMap is recommended for a
different setting (distant species, poor homology annotation). The low trajectory conservation is a direct caution for
using SAMap to align positions along a continuous axis.
