## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic (Results "Statistical model"; Methods "Negative binomial generalized additive model")
  - Per gene, an NB-GAM: Y_gi ~ NB(mu_gi, phi_g), log mu_gi = sum over lineages of s_gl(T_li) Z_li + U_i alpha_g + log N_i. Lineage-specific cubic smoothing splines s_gl with K basis functions (default K = 6), the same basis for all genes and lineages; coefficients penalized with a smoothing parameter chosen by generalized cross-validation in mgcv, shared across lineages so smoothers are comparable. Z = hard lineage assignment sampled from normalized TI weights (Supplementary Methods; results vary slightly by seed: 1,990-2,049 startVsEnd DE genes over 10 seeds). U = known cell-level covariates (e.g. batch, age, gender) as fixed effects; N_i = cell offset for depth. Zero inflation via observation weights from ZINB-WaVE.
  - Number of knots (Methods "Choosing an appropriate number of knots"): chosen by the user with evaluateK, which compares AIC across K on a random subset of genes; "We have deliberately chosen the AIC ... since the Bayesian information criterion (BIC) seemed to favor overly complex models". One K for the whole data set (4-8 in the examples). Knots are placed at quantiles of pseudotime.
  - Tests (Methods "Statistical inference"): Wald tests of linear contrasts C^T beta_g = 0 with a chi-squared null. Within lineage: associationTest (all smoother coefficients equal = flat), startVsEndTest (mean at start vs end; "a special case of a more general capability ... to compare the mean expression between any two regions of a given lineage"). Between lineages: diffEndTest (endpoints), patternTest (100 equally spaced points across each lineage; a screening test that picks up both pattern and mean differences), earlyDETest (patternTest restricted to the range between two user-specified knots, meant to enclose a branching event). Each test can be run per lineage/pair or as a global test; stage-wise testing with stageR (screening + confirmation, overall FDR). Ranking score combining two tests (patternTest high, diffEndTest low) to find transient differences (Methods "Case studies").
  - Clustering of fitted values (100 per lineage, standardized per gene) with RSEC (clusterExperiment); RSEC more stable than PAM over cell bootstraps (Supplementary Fig. 25).
- Null / calibration / significance: nominal chi-squared Wald p-values, BH FDR. The authors write that "we view the p-values simply as useful numerical summaries for ranking the genes" rather than with "strong probabilistic interpretations", and that stage-wise p-values "might still be too liberal since the same data are used for trajectory inference and differential expression". Pseudotime is treated as fixed and known (Discussion). Replicate unit = cell; there is no sample/replicate level in the model. In the bulk time-course case (Supplementary Note 2) three replicates per condition x six time points are simply observations in the GAM.
- Validation: dynverse simulations (Table 1): 10 cyclic data sets (dyngen, ~505 cells, 312-444 genes), 10 bifurcating and 1 multifurcating (dyntoy, 500/750 cells, 5,000 genes, 20% DE); FDP-TPR curves vs Monocle 3 Moran's I, BEAM, GPfates, ImpulseDE2 and supervised edgeR on end clusters (Fig. 3, Supplementary Figs. 6-13). The authors note the simulations are "relatively clean". tradeSeq_slingshot best; edgeR on true end clusters similar; BEAM, ImpulseDE2 and GPfates outperformed. Runtime: ImpulseDE2 >3.5 h on 100 cells (Fig. 4). Real data: Paul bone marrow (2,660 cells), Fletcher olfactory epithelium (616 cells, ZINB), Merrick adipocytes (8,071 cells), Kiselev bulk time course (89% overlap with per-time-point DESeq2).
- Plot types: knot lines on a fitted smoother (Fig. 1b); toy panels per test (Fig. 2); smoothers per lineage for top genes (Fig. 5c, Supplementary Fig. 28); clusters of fitted patterns (Fig. 1d, 5d); heatmap of top associationTest genes with clusters of activity regions (Fig. 6c); knot-delimited earlyDE plots (Fig. 6d); evaluateK AIC panels (Supplementary Figs. 4, 8, 15, 17, 22, 26); UpSet plots (Supplementary Figs. 30-31).
- Software: R/Bioconductor tradeSeq (http://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/tradeSeq.html; https://github.com/statOmics/tradeSeq); paper code https://github.com/statOmics/tradeSeqPaper.
- Limitations stated: pseudotime uncertainty ignored; pseudotime scales may differ between lineages (suggests dynamic time warping before patternTest); two-step ZINB fitting; double use of data for TI and DE.
- Limitations for OUR design: cells (units) are the replicates; a specimen can only enter as a fixed cell-level covariate, so between-condition tests at 2 vs 2 are confounded with specimen; quantile-placed knots follow unit density, which differs between specimens/conditions; chi-squared Wald p-values are not calibrated for specimen-level inference.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Our per-specimen NB splines with a library-size offset are already the tradeSeq model fitted per specimen. What transfers is the contrast menu on fitted curves (association/flat, start vs end, any two regions, endpoint, full-pattern, window between two knots) and the patternTest-high/diffEndTest-low ranking for transient differences, all to be evaluated on specimen-level curves with our relabeling calibration. Its own p-values, cell-level and explicitly "numerical summaries", do not transfer.
