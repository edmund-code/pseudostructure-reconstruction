## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic: for each pathway, cells from K groups are pooled in the space of the pathway's genes (log1p-normalised expression); a minimum non-bipartite matching is built on the pooled cells (minimising total Euclidean edge length, R package crossmatch); cross-counts a_st (edges joining group s and t) form a vector A; S = (A - E_H0 A)^T Cov_H0^-1 (A - E_H0 A) is distribution-free under H0 and compared with chi-square on K-choose-2 df; Q = -log(p) (STAR Methods "Graph-based test used in SCPA"; statistical theory in Mukherjee et al. JASA 2020, ref. 21). Default gene-set size filter 15-200 genes (15-500 in benchmarks).
- Null / calibration / significance: asymptotic chi-square p-values; replicate unit = cell (cells are the independent observations). No type I error / null calibration experiment reported; benchmarks are power curves (Fig. 1C,D) and counts/ranks of viral pathways in infected-vs-mock data (Supp. Fig. 1C-E).
- "Qval": the paper calls it the size of distribution change used for ranking, but it is -log p, so it depends on cell numbers; it is non-directional (direction is shown separately as mean pathway change in a volcano, Fig. 4D).
- Pseudotime use: slingshot trajectory via dyno; cells grouped onto the nearest of 4 milestones; the milestone groups enter the K-sample test as unordered categories (Fig. 4A,B; STAR Methods "Trajectory inference analysis and pseudotime SCPA").
- Replicates: none; the in vitro scRNA-seq resource came from PBMCs of one donor (STAR Methods "CD4+ and CD8+ T cell purification"); 0, 12, 24 h compared as 3 groups.
- Validation: Splatter simulation, 200-gene pathway, two groups, varying DE size and DE probability, vs fGSEA, iDEA, AUCell, GSVA, Vision, ssGSEA, z-score (Fig. 1B-D); four public virus-infection datasets; biological follow-up (IFNA qPCR, ELISA, CRISPR, Ifnar1-/- mice; PLA2 inhibition; DEFA1 flow cytometry).
- Plot types: Qval heatmaps across comparisons with k-means clusters (Fig. 3B, 4B, 4K, 5D); ranked Qval plot (Fig. 3E); Qval vs mean pathway change volcano (Fig. 4D); PCA of Qvals (Fig. 5E); variance of Qvals (Fig. 5F); mean pathway expression vs pseudotime (Fig. 4C).
- Software: R package SCPA (https://github.com/jackbibby1/SCPA; docs https://jackbibby1.github.io/SCPA), v1.1.0 in key resources.
- Limitations the authors state: limited benchmarking scenarios; viral-pathway benchmark imperfect (false positives possible); limited in vitro validation (Limitations of the Study).
- Limitations for OUR design: cell-level test (tubule units would be the "cells"), so with hundreds of units nearly any PT pathway differs between S1/S2/S3 bins; ignores the order along the axis; non-directional; no specimen model; Q grows with n.

## Fit to our design (revised)
Not usable as a test for us: unit-level, order-blind and sign-free, with no specimen model and no null calibration in the paper. Its one idea worth keeping is "distribution change without mean change" (Fig. 4D), which our per-gene curve summaries already cover better.
