## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Design: imaging study in male C57Bl/6 mice (8-15 weeks) plus OK and HK-2 cells. Ratiometric pH probes (pHrodo/Atto-647N-labelled lysozyme and a dual-labelled 20-aa peptide) are filtered and endocytosed in early PT (S1); intravital multiphoton imaging tracks pH through brush border -> early endosomes (3 min) -> late endosomes/large apical vacuoles (6 min) -> cathepsin-positive lysosomes (10 min). Lysotracker (taken up basolaterally) labels lysosomes in S2. S3 could not be imaged intravitally (too deep; Discussion limitations).
- Axial findings: S1 lysosomes are small, cathepsin-rich, sit just below the late-endosome band, make transient contacts with late endosomes and degrade filtered protein (lactoglobulin unquenching). S2 lysosomes are larger, dispersed, highly motile, contain lysosomal acid lipase (LAL), traffic basally to mitochondria-associated lipid droplets and extrude multilamellar bodies into the lumen. Immunostaining shows "a striking inverse relationship" of cathepsin L (highest in S1) and LAL (more prominent in OAT1-positive S2) along the PT (Fig. 7e; histogram of PT regions ordered from highest to lowest cathepsin signal, n = 3 mice, 20 tubules).
- Perturbations: hydroxychloroquine (HCQ) de-acidification disrupts megalin recycling and protein uptake in S1 (proteinuria) and re-routes S2 lysosomes to apical exocytosis with luminal lipid release (urine lipidomics); Lalistat-2 (LAL inhibitor) increases lipid vesicles.
- Statistics: one-way ANOVA or t-tests with n = 2-5 animals; ROIs from all animals merged to estimate means (so ROIs, not animals, are often the units).
- Plot types: intravital image sequences, temporal colour-coding of vesicle tracks, ratiometric bar charts by compartment, immunostaining panels, EM images, contact raster plots. No quantitative profile along measured PT length and no per-tubule (nephron-to-nephron) variation analysis.
- Software: Fiji, Imaris, ZEN, Ilastik, ScanImage, LION/web; no custom package.
- Limitations (authors): 2-D tracking; compartment identity by cross-reference; no in vivo pH calibration (qualitative pH); mouse only; S3 not visualised; acute effects only.
