## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model (Methods "Models and algorithm"; Supp. Text): per gene, a linear additive mixed model on LOG-NORMALISED expression (Gaussian, not NB): y = covariates (e.g. read depth, cell size) + shape-constrained spline + random intercept per individual (compound-symmetric block covariance) + noise. Growth/recession use quadratic I-spline bases with non-negative coefficients; peak/trough use cubic C-splines plus a linear term. Knots default to the number of time points (Supp. Fig. S17 shows power stable when knots are near the number of time points). Fitting by cone projection (R coneproj) after an eigen-transform; the variance ratio theta is estimated iteratively from a restricted likelihood (Supp. Text Algorithm 1).
- Test (Supp. Text "Statistical testing"): per pattern, B = (SSR0 - SSR1)/SSR0, whose null is a mixture of Beta distributions with mixing weights estimated by simulating y ~ MVN(0, I) through the cone projection; four pattern P values combined by the Cauchy (ACAT) rule; FDR by BH, and in real data by permuting "the time point variables" 5 times (Methods "Permutation strategy"; the text does not say whether cells or samples were permuted).
- Pattern call (Methods "Determining one of the temporal expression patterns"): Linear TDEseq picks the pattern by AIC; Mixed TDEseq by the largest B statistic. Hard assignment, no class probabilities.
- Two versions: Mixed TDEseq (random intercept) and Linear TDEseq (no random effect), recommended when individual heterogeneity is small.
- Calibration (Results; Fig. 1B, 1E; Supp. Figs S2-S4, S9, S11): simulations with Splatter, 4-6 time points, THREE samples per time point, 50-300 cells per sample. Mixed TDEseq and pseudobulk DESeq2 were calibrated at baseline; Linear TDEseq, tradeSeq, ImpulseDE2, Wilcoxon and edgeR were not. Mixed TDEseq was NOT calibrated with high UMI counts (Fig. S3B) or with large batch (sample-level) effects (Fig. 1E) unless combined with scMerge batch correction. Pseudobulk DESeq2/edgeR were more powerful with high UMI counts or few cells per sample (Figs S3-S4).
- Pattern accuracy at 5% FDR, baseline (Fig. 1D): Mixed TDEseq 99.0% growth, 100% recession, 80.4% peak, 43.6% trough (ImpulseDE2 73.1/83.5/39.3/21.3%). Discussion: Mixed TDEseq calls weak peaks/troughs as growth/recession; multi-modal patterns are poorly detected (Fig. S8B).
- Pseudotime input: with intertwined cells, Slingshot pseudotime can be fed to TDEseq; with many intertwined cells, pseudotime methods (tradeSeq, ImpulseDE2) beat time-point TDEseq; with pseudotime input, Linear TDEseq beat Mixed TDEseq and ImpulseDE2 but not tradeSeq (Fig. S12B-F; mouse liver Fig. S14G-I).
- Real data: Well-TEMP-seq drug time course (1 sample per time point), mouse hepatoblast differentiation (1-2 samples per stage, 345 cells), human LUAD epithelium (2-11 samples per stage), COVID-19 NK cells (3-8 patients per stage); more temporal genes and GO terms than comparators.
- Plots: z-scored pattern heatmaps (Figs 2D, 3D, 4D, 5E); QQ plots; power vs FDR curves; Venn of detections with GO counts; UMAP feature plots; GO bubble plots per pattern.
- Software: R package TDEseq, https://github.com/fanyue322/TDEseq (Zenodo 10869078).
- Limitations stated: four patterns only (multi-modal hard); weak peak/trough mislabelled by Mixed TDEseq; LAMM inference hard above about 6,000 cells (down-sampling suggested); batch correction can introduce extra variation. Limitations for OUR design: Gaussian log model, not NB with offset; random intercept only (no random shape); no between-condition test; calibration shown with 3 samples per stage, not 2. An independent benchmark (PseudotimeDE-fast 2025, Fig. 1c and Fig. S2) found TDEseq null P values miscalibrated in a single-sample pseudotime simulation and TDEseq too slow for >= 50,000 cells.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Good source for the four-shape vocabulary and shape-constrained bases (also available as scam-type NB smooths). Not a test to adopt: Gaussian on log data, random intercept only, hard labels, weak peak/trough accuracy, no condition comparison, and calibration not shown below 3 samples per stage.
