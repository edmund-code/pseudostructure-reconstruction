## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic: a multimodal atlas. snCv3 (single-nucleus), scCv3 (single-cell) and SNARE-seq2 data from 58 reference tissues (35 donors) and 52 diseased tissues (36 patients); >400,000 nuclei/cells; 100 clusters annotated to 77 subclasses; 51 canonical cell types and 28 altered states (cycling, adaptive, transitioning, degenerative) (Main "Constructing a kidney cellular atlas", "Reference and altered states"). Clustering with Pagoda2 (infomap), then Seurat v4. Spatial: Slide-seq2 (6 individuals, 67 pucks; RCTD deconvolution), Visium (22 individuals, 23 sections; Seurat transfer scores), 3D imaging (15 individuals, ~1.5 million cells, 14 niches).
- PT position: PT-S1, PT-S2, PT-S3 are discrete subclasses (Extended Data Fig. 9b "PT subclasses (PT-S1-S3, aPT)"); PT-S1/PT-S2 "could not be accurately resolved" in scCv3 and were kept merged (Methods "Integrating snCv3 and scCv3 datasets"). Regional (cortex/outer/inner medulla) annotation comes from dissection.
- Continuous axes: (i) Fig. 2c, a heat map of Slide-seq cell-type frequencies along the corticomedullary axis (three individuals), i.e. whole-kidney depth, not position within the PT; (ii) injury/repair pseudotime: Slingshot on PT and TAL cells with S1-S3 (PT) and M-TAL as end points; per gene a cubic-spline GAM on pseudotime compared with an intercept-only model by F-test, BH; a conditional-smooth interaction for AKI vs CKD; WGCNA modules on smoothed expression; CellAlign to align human and mouse IRI trajectories (Methods "Pseudotime analysis of PT and TAL cells", "Gene module detection").
- Null / calibration: Fisher's exact tests on spot proportions (Fig. 3a,c); permutation of labels for Slide-seq proximity enrichment (2,500 permutations); cell-level GAM F-tests (cells are the units); AKI vs CKD pseudobulk DESeq2 per cell type (Cacoa); Kaplan-Meier/Cox for clinical scores (NEPTUNE, ERCB).
- Replicates: many donors, but most expression analyses pool cells; patient pseudobulk used only for AKI-vs-CKD DE and sample clustering.
- Validation: cross-platform integration metrics; mapping to mouse IRI (Kirita 2020); IF and RNA ISH; clinical association in independent cohorts.
- Plot types: UMAPs; binned heat map of cell-type frequencies along the corticomedullary axis (Fig. 2c); dot plots of markers; Visium transfer-score maps and pie charts; heat maps of smoothed expression along repair pseudotime with module blocks (Fig. 5b, Ext. Data Fig. 9c); RNA-velocity streamlines; CellChat circle plots; Kaplan-Meier curves.
- Software: Pagoda2, Seurat 4, Slingshot, mgcv-style GAM, WGCNA, scVelo, CellOracle, CellChat, RCTD/Giotto; figure code at github.com/KPMP/Cell-State-Atlas-2022.
- Limitations (authors): heterogeneous sample sources across technologies; clinical correlations on small samples. For us: PT position is discrete (S1/S2/S3 subclasses, some merged); the only continuous trajectories are injury/repair pseudotimes that mix PT segment end points with injury state; the axis heat map is whole-kidney depth.

## Fit to our design
Context only. It is the standard human kidney reference: discrete PT-S1/S2/S3 subclasses and a whole-kidney corticomedullary binning. It supports the claim that a continuous within-PT anatomical axis in tissue is not done, and it is a reference for human PT segment markers and adaptive-PT (aPT: VCAM1, HAVCR1, DCDC2) signatures relevant to our AKI mice.
