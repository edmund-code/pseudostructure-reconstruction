## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic: from 208 curated ArrayExpress/GEO submissions (568 experiments, 2,652 arrays; the methods count 219 submissions and 581 experiments before QC, 575 after) of < 24 h perturbations of 11 pathways (EGFR, MAPK, PI3K, VEGF, JAK-STAT, TGFb, TNFa, NFkB, Hypoxia, p53/DNA damage, Trail), compute per-experiment z-scores of perturbed vs basal arrays, then per gene regress z-scores on a signed pathway-perturbation indicator matrix (with cross-talk coded: EGFR experiments also set MAPK; TNFa also NFkB). Keep the top 100 genes per pathway by P value; their fitted z-scores are the weights; all other weights 0. Pathway score = expression matrix x weight matrix, then each pathway scaled to mean 0, SD 1 across samples (Methods, "PROGENy scores").
- Null / calibration: no per-sample test; downstream associations use ANOVA / Cox models with FDR across samples (tumours, cell lines). Leave-one-out model building for perturbation recall.
- Replicates: samples (tumours, cell lines) are the units in downstream models.
- Validation: leave-one-out recall of perturbed pathway (p < 1e-10 except Trail); 10 held-out experiments with Western-blot activity; new HEK293 perturbations with phospho-protein readouts (Fig. 2b-c); stability under bootstrapping of input experiments (Fig. 2d); TCGA driver mutations (EGFR amp -> EGFR/MAPK; TP53 mut -> lower p53; VHL mut -> hypoxia, FDR < 1e-200), GDSC drug response (178 associations at 10% FDR, 265 drugs x 805 lines), TCGA survival. Compared with GO/Reactome GSVA, SPIA, Pathifier, PARADIGM, SPEED, Gatza signatures. A single experiment's z-scores performed no better than random; 20-50 signatures per pathway were needed for a plateau.
- Key rationale (Fig. 1a; Results; Discussion): responsive genes overlap little with the genes encoding the pathway's signalling proteins (Supp. Fig. 3), so pathway-member expression is not pathway activity; footprints capture post-translational activation through downstream targets.
- Plots: workflow; heatmap of scores vs perturbed pathway (Fig. 2a); volcano of mutation-pathway associations (Fig. 3a); method x pathway comparison tiles; drug-association counts vs FDR; violin stratification of IC50; survival association matrix, volcano and Kaplan-Meier curves (Figs 4-5).
- Software: R/Bioconductor package progeny; analysis code https://github.com/saezlab/footprints.
- Limitations (authors): consensus footprints may miss context-specific (tissue-specific) responses; perturbation data exist for fewer pathways than gene-set resources. For us: footprints were learned in human (mostly cancer) cell lines on microarrays, from acute (< 24 h) perturbations; a mouse model is not part of this paper; scores are scaled across the samples included, so they are relative to the pool.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Useful as a membership-free signalling readout along PT (hypoxia is a natural candidate in S3 and in IR). Transfer to adult kidney epithelium in situ is untested, the weights are human, and acute-perturbation footprints may not equal steady-state positional programs. Score per unit, fit per-specimen curves, and treat as descriptive.
