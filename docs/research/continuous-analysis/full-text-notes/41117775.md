## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic: NOT a regression model and NOT subsampling. PseudotimeDE-fast "replaces the subsampling-and-permutation procedure with a direct, deterministic statistical test" (Implementation): for each gene, a test of independence between the pseudotime vector and the gene's count vector using the Bergsma-Dassios sign covariance tau* (a quartet-based extension of Kendall's tau; tau* = 0 iff independent), computed with an O(Mn) algorithm over unique expression ranks (Supp. S1), with P values from its known limiting null distribution (Nandy et al. 2016). Pseudotime and expression are both treated as random variables.
- Null / calibration: asymptotic null of tau*. Calibration shown only by simulation: scDesign3 data (2,000 genes, 20% DE; 1,000-100,000 cells) trained on dentate-gyrus neurogenesis with Slingshot pseudotime; QQ plots and KS tests of null P values (Fig. 1c, Fig. S2). Only PseudotimeDE-fast and the original subsampling-permutation PseudotimeDE were well calibrated; tradeSeq, TDEseq and PseudotimeDE-fix deviated (e.g. KS P for TDEseq 5.2e-23 at 1,000 cells; tradeSeq 1.8e-155; Fig. S2). The authors note the simulation has high signal-to-noise so double dipping (Neufeld 2024) is mild, and suggest combining with ClusterDE's synthetic null if double dipping is a concern (Results).
- Replicates / covariates: none. Cells are the observations; "does not natively adjust for covariates such as batch effects or sequencing depth; users should therefore correct for confounders prior to analysis" (Discussion). Single condition only (tests change along pseudotime, not between conditions).
- Validation: runtime (at 10,000 cells, 124 s CPU, about 300x faster than tradeSeq; TDEseq did not finish within 48 h at >= 50,000 cells); FDR and power comparable to other methods with slight power loss for very sparse genes (Fig. 1d, Fig. S1, S3); real alveolar fibroblast data (35,096 cells; 63% of its DE genes shared with both PseudotimeDE-fix and tradeSeq) (Fig. 1e-f).
- Plots: expression vs pseudotime for example genes with tau* and P (Fig. 1a); runtime curves; QQ plots of null P values; FDR/power bars; UpSet of DE overlaps.
- Software: R with Rcpp, https://github.com/dsong-lab/PseudotimeDE (MIT).
- Limitations stated: no covariates (needs a conditional tau*, not yet developed); double dipping. Limitations for OUR design: no exposure offset, no specimen terms, no between-condition test; rank-based, so it tests monotone-or-not association, not shape or location.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Low. It is a fast single-condition, cell-level, covariate-free independence test; our units need an exposure offset and specimen terms, and our questions are between conditions. Its main value for us is the 2025 null benchmark showing tradeSeq and TDEseq P values miscalibrated along pseudotime.
