## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Data: Drop-seq scRNA-seq of whole kidneys (n = 2 male mice; 5,675 cells) and of manually dissected cortex (3,046), outer medulla (2,392) and inner medulla (889 cells; pooled from 3 mice); bulk RNA-seq of cortex, outer and inner medulla (n = 5 each); snRNA-seq of mid-kidney slices from Grhl2 collecting-duct knockouts and controls in two experiments (Methods; Results first section).
- Which cells: the spatial reconstruction is done for collecting-duct principal cells (CD-PCs) only. Regional origin explained more HVG variance in TL, CD-PC and CD-IC than in PT and TAL (Fig. 2D, one-way ANOVA), and a glmnet multinomial classifier trained on dissected-region labels reached > 80% accuracy for CD-PC and TL, lower for other cell types (Results; Supp. Fig. 3 not read).
- Ordering: novoSpaRc run de novo on 753 CD-PCs from dissected samples with 872 CD-PC-specific HVGs onto 100 one-dimensional positions; the region labels are not used to fit the ordering but to check it afterwards (Fig. 3A-B), and the result was replicated on Ransick et al.'s data. Leave-one-HVG-out re-sorting showed that 48% of HVGs strongly affect the order (Fig. 3D). snRNA-seq nuclei are later placed by maximal correlation to the sorted profiles (500 positions) (Fig. 6A).
- Expression along the axis: the "sdge" matrix (expression x cell-to-position probabilities). 85 of 104 location-specific markers found in Ransick's data were confirmed, and their heatmap showed "a smooth transition of cell states" rather than clusters; "cellular subtypes of CD-PCs continuously transition into one another, instead of forming distinct sharp gene-expression boundaries" (Fig. 3E; Results).
- Gradient genes: per-gene least-squares linear regression on the 100 reconstructed positions; cut-offs on slope (75th percentile), P < 5e-10 and |r| > 0.6 give 704 increasing and 334 decreasing genes (Fig. 4A); Enrichr KEGG enrichment (Fig. 4B). The P values treat the 100 smoothed positions as independent observations.
- Osmogenes: 14 of 90 literature osmogenes among increasing genes (P < 0.001 for overrepresentation); 12 also rise in bulk RNA-seq; 8 hypoxia genes similarly (Fig. 4C).
- Validation: RNAscope for Scnn1g, Hsd11b2 (decreasing), Cryab (increasing), Fxyd4 (complex), semi-quantified on 5 collecting ducts in each of 7 regions (Fig. 5).
- Phenotype: in Grhl2 CD knockouts (known lower medullary osmolality), osmogene expression along the axis, shown as sliding averages over 150 of 500 positions with 95% CI, is lower towards the medulla in both experiments (Fig. 6B). No statistical test of slope or difference is reported.
- Plot types: tSNE; HVG heatmaps by region; variance-explained bars; position-density curves of dissected-region cells (Fig. 3B); marker heatmaps along positions; COM-style "location of maximum" sorted heatmap (Fig. 3E); regression-P-sorted min-max heatmaps with mean line and 95% CI (Fig. 4A); ISH vs in-silico profiles (Fig. 5); sliding-average KO vs control lines (Fig. 6B).
- Software: R, Seurat 2.3.1, glmnet 2.0-16; novoSpaRc; code at www.mdc-berlin.de/kidneyspatial.zip; Shiny viewer https://shiny.mdc-berlin.de/KidneySpatial/; GEO GSE145690.
- Limitations (authors): low Drop-seq depth; not all datasets preserve osmotic responses (dissociation protocol matters); the proximity-implies-similarity assumption "may not retain validity ... in disease settings such as ischemia"; Grhl2 could act directly on some genes.

## Review check
- c-spatial-gradients.md:32 (row #12), "Cortico-medullary axis reconstructed from dissociated kidney sc/snRNA-seq." - VERDICT: misses an important point: only collecting-duct principal cells were ordered, and PT carried the least regional signal. Replacement: "Cortico-medullary axis reconstructed for collecting-duct principal cells only; PT and TAL carried the least regional information."
- c:32, "novoSpaRc (optimal transport) embeds cells on 100 one-dimensional positions, trained on dissected cortex / outer medulla / inner medulla." - VERDICT: wrong in one word. novoSpaRc was run de novo; dissected-region labels were used to validate the order, not to train it (a separate glmnet classifier was trained on the labels). Replacement: "novoSpaRc orders CD-PCs de novo onto 100 positions; cells from dissected cortex, outer and inner medulla are used to check the order."
- c:32, "Genes are placed on a min-max-normalised heatmap sorted by linear-regression P value." - VERDICT: correct (Fig. 4A).
- c:32, "Lines are a sliding average over 150 positions with 95% CI." - VERDICT: correct for Fig. 6B (150 of 500 positions); Fig. 4A mean lines also have 95% CI.
- c:32, "12 osmo-responsive genes follow the osmolality gradient." - VERDICT: correct (14 enriched; 12 confirmed in bulk).
- c:32, "Grhl2 ... 'flattened expression gradient' (n=2; per the PMC text, no formal slope test)." - VERDICT: correct; now verified from the full text (two experiments, no test).
- c:32, Replicates "2 KO vs controls; no formal test" - VERDICT: correct.
- c:32, Software "R (Seurat, glmnet); novoSpaRc (Python)" - VERDICT: correct.
- c:32, What it adds, "kidney precedent for a gradient-flattening phenotype ... We can test slope or range per specimen, which Hinze did not do formally." - VERDICT: correct, but misses three points. (1) Hinze is also the kidney precedent for "continuous beats discrete": CD-PC 'subtypes' were shown to be a continuum, and the authors argue that categorisation can miss continuous state transitions. (2) That "smooth transition" is read from sdge = expression x novoSpaRc's entropy-regularised cell-to-position probabilities, which averages each cell over neighbouring positions and so smooths any step by construction (see the novoSpaRc note, 31748748); the continuum claim is therefore weaker evidence than it looks. (3) Their gradient P values come from regressing 100 reconstructed positions, which overstates evidence; our specimen-level tests are the fix.
- c:68 (Cross-cutting), "a 'flattened gradient' judged by eye (#12)" - VERDICT: correct.
- c:85 (Gaps), kidney work "cortico-medullary across segments (#12)" - VERDICT: imprecise: Hinze's axis is within one cell type (CD-PC), not across segments. Replacement: "cortico-medullary within the collecting duct (#12)".
- c:90 (Notes), "Hinze 2021 (#12) method details ... come from a WebFetch summary" - now verified from the full text, with the "trained on" correction above.

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
Closest kidney precedent for an expression-derived 1-D axis with a continuous-vs-subtype conclusion and a condition effect read as a flattened gradient. Its statistics are weak (P values over reconstructed positions; KO effect by eye, n = 2), which is where our per-specimen slope/range tests add value. Note its own warning that proximity-based ordering may fail under ischaemia, relevant to placing IR units on our axis.
