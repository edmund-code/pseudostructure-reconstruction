## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Design: 10x Visium (55 um spots; "roughly the size of a tubular cross-section"). One human reference nephrectomy (59-year-old woman) and, in mouse, one sham, one IRI (bilateral clamp 22 min) and one CLP kidney, all taken at a single time point, 6 h after surgery (Methods "Murine models"; Fig. 4 legend "n = 1 murine sample per model"). It is not a time course. scRNA-seq reference: 2 sham, 1 IRI (30 min clamp), 1 LPS (public GSE154107).
- Model / statistic: Space Ranger graph clustering of spots; Seurat 3.1 label transfer from sn/scRNA-seq clusters (each spot labelled by its highest transfer score); SPOTlight deconvolution; an immune "transfer score" after suppressing epithelial clusters; colocalisation odds ratios with Fisher's exact tests; DEGs by Seurat FindMarkers (Wilcoxon) between hand-drawn "low-expression" and "preserved" regions or between spot groups; ReactomePA/clusterProfiler enrichment (Methods "Statistics").
- Key findings: human spot clusters match histology (97.6% overall; Fig. 1K). In mouse IRI at 6 h: Havcr1 localised to the outer stripe; Lcn2 diffusely up (Results, Supp. Fig. 2). Regions of low total counts in cortex with reduced nuclei (attributed to oedema) (Fig. 5). Two injured PT scRNA-seq clusters: Havcr1-high with S3 markers (PT S3-OS), and a fibrinogen-high (Fga/Fgb/Fgg) cluster with S1/S2 markers (Results "Mapping of single-cell sequencing clusters"). S3 was split into a cortical S3 (S3-C) and an outer-stripe S3 (S3-OS) cluster. Neutrophil signature colocalised with PT S3-OS spots in the outer stripe of the medulla; Atf3 was higher in S3-OS spots that colocalised with neutrophils, localised to the outer stripe, and was confirmed by ATF3 immunofluorescence (n = 3) and an Atf3-high S3-OS subcluster in scRNA-seq (Figs 7-8). In CLP: infiltrating macrophage signature in outer cortex with Mdk in cortical PT (Fig. 9). CODEX validation: 52.2% of neutrophils in IRI in the outer stripe; 80.2% of infiltrating macrophages in CLP in the outer cortex (Fig. 10).
- Null / calibration: spot-level Wilcoxon and Fisher tests within single sections; no specimen replication for the spatial data (n = 1 per model). P < 0.05 called significant.
- Validation: histology concordance, CODEX, ATF3 immunofluorescence (n = 3 per group).
- Plots: spot cluster overlays on H&E; UMAPs; transfer-label maps; SPOTlight pie charts; volcano plots and pathway bars for region comparisons; colocalisation odds-ratio dot plots; Atf3/Mdk feature maps.
- Software: Space Ranger 1.0.0, Seurat 3.1 (SCTransform, label transfer), PAGODA2, SPOTlight, ReactomePA, clusterProfiler, CODEX-MAV/FIJI. No Giotto and no Monocle2 in this paper.
- Limitations stated: 55 um spots overlie several cells; about 5,000 spots per capture area; the work is descriptive. Limitations for OUR design: one animal per model, one early time point (6 h), spot-level tests; anatomical position is discrete (cortex / outer stripe / S3-C vs S3-OS clusters), not continuous.

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
A precedent for spatial localisation of early IR injury to the outer-stripe S3 PT, with Atf3 and neutrophils, but on n = 1 mouse per model, one 6-h time point, 55-um Visium spots and discrete regions. It gives candidate positional controls (S3-OS Atf3, outer-stripe Havcr1, fibrinogen-high injured S1/S2) but no statistical template for us.
