## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic: fuzzy c-means (FCM) clustering of gene expression profiles. Each gene i gets a membership value mu_ij in [0,1] for every cluster j (Methodology). Users choose the number of clusters c and the fuzzifier m; varying both probes cluster stability (Software input). Output is the partition matrix of memberships; high-membership "cluster cores" (alpha-cores, vignette 4.2, e.g. alpha = 0.7) define tightly co-expressed genes.
- Preprocessing (vignette only, not in the paper): filter genes with >25% missing, fill NAs (mean or kNN), no variance filter recommended, then `standardise()` to mean 0, SD 1 per gene "so that vectors of genes with similar changes in expression are close in Euclidean space" (vignette 3.3). The vignette says standardisation does not replace normalisation.
- Null / calibration / significance: none. No P values. The fuzzifier m can be set so that random data are not clustered (`partcoef`, or `mestimate` after Schwaemmle & Jensen 2010; vignette 4.1). The cluster number is chosen with `cselection` (empty clusters appear) or Dmin (minimum centroid distance), or by GO relevance.
- Replicates: not modelled. Vignette 4 and 7.3: "replicated arrays in the exprs matrix are treated as independent by the mfuzz function i.e. they should be averagered prior to clustering or placed into different distinct ExpressionSet objects."
- Validation: none in the note beyond the yeast cell-cycle example (Cho et al., 17 time points) and a citation to Futschik & Carlisle 2005 for noise robustness. Also notes use on protein phosphorylation time series.
- Plots (Fig. 1): per-cluster temporal profiles with lines coloured by membership (`mfuzz.plot`, red = high, green = low in the paper's figure); a "global clustering structure" plot: cluster centroids on principal components with lines showing cluster overlap/coupling (`overlap.plot`; coupling V_kl = mean over genes of mu_ik mu_il, vignette 6).
- Software: R/Bioconductor packages Mfuzz and Mfuzzgui (Tcl/Tk GUI), GPL-2; URL in paper http://itb1.biologie.hu-berlin.de/~futschik/software/R/Mfuzz/index.html (vignette now gives http://mfuzz.sysbiolab.eu).
- Limitations stated: GUI needs Tcl/Tk; choice of c is "challanging, especially for short time series" (vignette). Limitations for us: clusters standardised profiles only, so it is a descriptive grouping with no uncertainty from specimens; replicates must be averaged or analysed separately; z-scoring inflates noise in flat genes (Mfuzz argues FCM down-weights them through low membership).
- TCseq (vignette, same review row): designed for epigenomic time courses (ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq); differential analysis between time points uses the edgeR NB GLM; `timeclust` offers hard (hierarchical, pam, kmeans) or soft (fuzzy cmeans) clustering with an optional `standardize = TRUE` z-score; `timeclustplot` colour-codes membership.

## Fit to our design (revised after reading the full text)
A descriptive add-on only. Applied to per-specimen fitted curves (z-scored, or rescaled to [0,1]), FCM memberships and alpha-cores could show genes between two shape programs and how stable a cluster is to m and c. It gives no inference; specimen agreement would have to be checked by clustering each specimen separately and comparing memberships.
