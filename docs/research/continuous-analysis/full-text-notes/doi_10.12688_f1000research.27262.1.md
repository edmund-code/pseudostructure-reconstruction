## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Model / statistic:
  - Spline model per gene: create_moanin_model(..., degrees_of_freedom = 6) in the worked example; default formula ~Group:ns(Timepoint, df) + Group + 0, i.e. a separate natural spline per group (p. 9). df 6 is the value chosen in the example; the text does not call it a package default.
  - DE: DE_timepoints (per-time-point limma contrasts) and DE_timecourse (limma test of the whole spline mean-function contrast between groups, e.g. "M-K"); voom weights for RNA-seq, off for this microarray data (pp. 10-13). 30-70% of genes DE.
  - Log-fold-change summaries (Table 4): timely (per time point), sum, abs_sum, max, min, and "timecourse" = sign(mean lfc) x mean |lfc| (formula p. 15); authors warn the direction is unreliable when a gene is not consistently up or down.
  - Filtering before clustering: Fisher-combined p-values across contrasts (adjusted) < 0.05 AND max |lfc| > 2 in some condition and time point (5,521 genes) (p. 18).
  - Splines k-means (p. 19): fit splines per gene; rescale each gene's fitted values to [0, 1]; k-means on the rescaled fitted values. The rescaling is per gene ACROSS ALL GROUPS jointly (centroids span all samples), so between-group differences within a gene are kept (cluster 6: treatments change, control flat; Fig. 18).
  - Score and label (p. 21): goodness-of-fit score in [0, 1] of each gene to each centroid after a best affine fit per gene and group; all genes labelled if their best score passes a cutoff (50th percentile of all scores by default); 19,772 genes labelled.
  - Choosing k (pp. 25-32): elbow (WCSS), silhouette, bootstrap/subsample of GENES (B = 20-25) with consensus matrices (Fig. 21), model explorer (Ben-Hur 2001) and consensus-CDF AUC (Monti 2003). These are diagnostics for k and stability; most stable k was 2 (up vs down), which the authors reject as too coarse.
  - Enrichment per cluster: KEGG with KEGGprofile; GO BP with topGO (pp. 33-35).
- Null / calibration: limma moderated tests with BH; Fisher combination of p-values (reviewer Thiebaut notes Fisher's method assumes independence). No null for clusters.
- Replicates: bulk biological replicates per group x time point (3 mice) are observations in limma. Footnote 1 and reviewer 3 state longitudinal (repeated-measures) designs are not handled; that would need random effects.
- Validation: one case study (Shoemaker 2015 mouse lung influenza microarray: 5 groups, 14 time points, 209 samples, 39,544 probes). No simulation or benchmark.
- Plots: PCA and sample-correlation QC; per-time-point DE counts; volcano with time-course lfc; spline fits per group per gene; centroid panels; raw vs rescaled heatmaps side by side (Fig. 18); consensus matrices; model-explorer CDFs; score histograms.
- Software: R/Bioconductor moanin (+ timecoursedata); workflow code GitHub NelleV/2019timecourse-rnaseq-pipeline.
- Limitations: authors - choice of k is ambiguous on real data. Reviewer Love - rescaling to [0, 1] works only because genes were pre-filtered to small p and large lfc; otherwise rescaling by the range amplifies noise. For OUR design: per-unit repeated measures within specimens are not modelled; Gaussian/limma on log scale, no NB with exposure offset.

## Review check
- a-gene-dynamics.md:41 - "A spline fit per gene and spline-contrast DE between groups. Several log-fold-change summaries across time (per timepoint, cumulative, overall). 'Splines k-means': rescale fitted splines to [0,1], then run k-means, which clusters by shape and ignores level. GO/KEGG enrichment per cluster." - VERDICT: correct.
- a-gene-dynamics.md:41 - "Replicates? Yes (bulk replicates)"; "R / Bioconductor moanin" - VERDICT: correct (cross-sectional replicates only; longitudinal not handled).
- a-gene-dynamics.md:41 - "Clustering fitted curves rescaled to [0,1] removes level differences between the probe panels. Mouse and human orthologs can then fall into shared shape clusters." - VERDICT: misses an important point. moanin rescales each gene once across all groups, so between-group level differences survive the rescaling (that is how its cluster 6 separates treated from control). Removing the panel offset needs a per-species rescaling, which is our modification, not moanin's. The authors' reviewer also notes range rescaling amplifies noise unless genes are pre-filtered for strong change.
  - Replacement: "Rescaling each fitted curve to [0,1] removes gene-level scale. moanin rescales across all groups together, so group level differences remain. To drop the probe-panel offset we would rescale each species separately, and only for genes with a strong positional change, because range rescaling amplifies noise in flat genes."
- a-gene-dynamics.md:94,97 (idea 5: cluster [0,1]-rescaled fitted curves, moanin) - VERDICT: correct, same caveat on pre-filtering.
- b-continuous-pathways.md:22 - "moanin fits natural splines per group (default df 6) through limma. It runs 'timecourse' contrasts, clusters the rescaled fitted curves with spline k-means and bootstrap consensus, then runs GO/KEGG enrichment per cluster." - VERDICT: mostly correct; two inaccuracies. (1) df 6 is the worked example's choice, not stated as a default. (2) Bootstrap/consensus resamples genes and is used to choose k and judge stability; the final clusters come from one k-means run plus goodness-of-fit labelling.
  - Replacement: "moanin fits a natural spline per group (df 6 in the worked example) and tests whole-curve contrasts with limma. It clusters genes by k-means on fitted curves rescaled to [0,1], labels all genes by a goodness-of-fit score, uses gene bootstraps and consensus matrices to choose k, and runs GO/KEGG enrichment per cluster."
- b-continuous-pathways.md:22 - "moanin: yes (bulk replicates)"; weakness "a hypergeometric test per cluster throws away the position order inside each cluster" - VERDICT: correct (KEGGprofile/topGO over-representation).
- b-continuous-pathways.md:131 - "moanin has no PMID that we could find. Its description comes from the F1000Research page (DOI above)." - VERDICT: correct (v1 has "2 approved with reservations"; the full PDF was read in this pass).

## Fit to our design (2-4 lines, revised after reading the full text)
Useful for two small pieces: the goodness-of-fit score that assigns every gene to a shape cluster with a fit quality, and gene-bootstrap consensus to show cluster stability. Its shape clustering keeps between-group levels unless each species is rescaled separately, and it should run only on genes with a strong positional change. Its limma models treat samples as independent and do not model units nested in specimens.
