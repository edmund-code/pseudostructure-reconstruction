## What the paper actually does (from the full text)
- Scope: 14 SVG methods (Moran's I, Spanve, scGCO, SpaGCN, SpaGFT, Sepal, SpatialDE, SpatialDE2, SPARK, SPARK-X, BOOST-GP, GPcounts, nnSVG, SOMDE; Table 1) on simulations built with scDesign3 from 50 real datasets (9 technologies, 17-18 tissues; NB GP marginal fits, spatial mean shuffled and mixed with weight alpha, 21 alpha levels), plus DLPFC/OSCC/HER2 domain-detection data and spatial ATAC-seq (abstract: "96 spatial datasets and 6 metrics").
- Metrics: ranking accuracy (Kendall correlation between estimated and true spatial variability; SPARK-X 0.88, SpatialDE2 0.81, nnSVG 0.80, Moran's I 0.76; Fig. 2a), auPRC, calibration, memory/time, clustering ARI with top-2000 SVGs vs HVGs, ATAC CHAOS score; overall average rank (Fig. 7): SPARK-X 4.3, SpaGFT 5.4, Moran's I third.
- Calibration protocol (Methods "Benchmarking statistical calibration"): ONE 10x Visium mouse olfactory bulb dataset (1,185 spots; genes expressed in >= 500 spots), spots permuted to destroy spatial structure; Q-Q plots and Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance to uniform (Fig. 3); repeated on one Xenium colon cancer dataset (Supp. Fig. S3). SpatialDE2 and Sepal excluded (no P values).
- Calibration result, as stated in Results: SPARK and SPARK-X well calibrated (attributed to Cauchy combination); SIX methods (SpatialDE, Spanve, SOMDE, scGCO, nnSVG, BOOST-GP) gave over-conservative P values; FOUR (SpaGFT, GPcounts, SpaGCN, Moran's I) failed to control type I error (the sentence says they "overestimated the p-values", which contradicts "failing to control type I errors"; the Q-Q direction is in Fig. 3a). The Background and Discussion summarise this as "all methods except for SPARK and SPARK-X produced inflated p-values" / "poorly calibrated", which does not match the detailed split. Guideline: choose SVGs by a fixed rank (e.g. top 2000) rather than by P value.
- Replicates: not addressed; every method and the null are single-sample; the null is spot permutation (independence), not between-specimen variation.
- Plot types: heat map of Kendall correlation per method x dataset; pattern-specific boxplots; Q-Q plots and K-S bars; scalability lines; ARI rank heat map; overall rank heat map.
- Software: benchmark code github.com/pinellolab/SVG_Benchmarking; Open Problems task github.com/openproblems-bio/task_spatially_variable_genes; Zenodo 16700772.
- Limitations (authors): simulation needs pre-selected spatially variable genes (possible bias); rotation invariance not tested. For us: calibration rests on one Visium dataset; the null shuffles spots within a section, so it says nothing about specimen-level (pseudoreplication) calibration.

## Fit to our design
Low as a method source; useful as a citation that SVG P values cannot be trusted without calibration and that simple baselines (Moran's I) rank well. It does not inform specimen-level inference.
